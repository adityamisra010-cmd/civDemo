## Pre-committed readings — which fired (§7.13)

**R1 (volume): the "near zero" branch FIRED.** Driven founded dev world (uniform 40/35/25
sector mix, 100 turns = 100 decades): traded 0 units of every good against per-decade
production in the tens of thousands; pair-goods over deadband: 97 crossings in 100 turns.
The throttle, MEASURED (not guessed): every crossing is BRONZE, whose high-side price sits
at the band ceiling — and every one has seller stock 0 and both market scales on the floor,
so q\* ≈ 0.05–0.27 floors to zero (and there is nothing to move regardless: bronze production
is 156 units per century, economy-wide). Every abundant good's inter-settlement gap stays
BELOW its deadband because the uniform drive makes settlements near-identical — no
comparative advantage, no arbitrage, which is the reference-class result for bulk goods at
overland costs. **Structural cause, named at certification (director ruling): this is the
FOUNDING-VARIATION queue item wearing a new face.** The D-025 equal-split endowment's lockstep
history (raised T2.9/T2.10, confirmed T2.13 — 11 of 12 settlements gaining artisans in the same
decade with near-identical counts) is the mechanical reason settlements have no comparative
advantage and therefore nothing to arbitrage; the queue entry now records that consequence —
founding uniformity blocks observable inter-settlement exchange and everything downstream of
trade volume. So neither "the deadband binds" nor "f binds" alone is the honest answer:
**the deadband binds for every good that exists in quantity, and the one good with a gap has
no inventory to arbitrage.** Per the pre-commitment: reported, and NOTHING was tuned to
manufacture flow. The mechanism itself is proven live by the acceptance battery (hand-built
gaps trade, cease, scale and conserve exactly).

**R2 (unbounded granary): the mechanical half is structural.** Grain's price is pinned at
1.0 everywhere (D-033), so its pairwise gap is zero and grain NEVER trades through this
mechanism — measured 0 in every rig and pinned non-vacuously. The grain price spread across
