## §1 — THE VARIANCE DEFECT (confirmed; the blocking one)

`HarvestWeatherSystem` builds the regional field with **self-weight `w_ii = 1.0`**, so `regional[i]`
*contains* `local[i]`, and then blends them as if independent:

```
innovation = √k·regional_i + √(1−k)·local_i
Var(innovation) = 1 + 2√(k(1−k))/√sumSq_i        ← not 1
```

Three lenses derived this independently from the code; a verifier re-derived it a fourth time before
reading their algebra, and all four agree. The decisive control is the **k-sweep**, which nothing in
the finding chain had until the verifier ran it:

| k | 0.0 | 0.3 | 0.5 | **0.6 (shipped)** | 1.0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sdLog ratio | 0.9996× | 1.2240× | 1.2422× | **1.2377×** | 0.9980× |
| E[mult] | 1.00000 | 1.02084 | 1.02272 | **1.02219** | 0.99893 |

**Exactly correct at both endpoints, maximal in between.** The defect is the blend's cross-term and
nothing else — not the kernel, not the AR(1), not Box–Muller. The mean sits **6.8 to 45.7 standard
errors above 1.0**, so it is not noise.

**Live impact, 32 seeds, matched control (σ=0 consumes identical RNG draws):**
total grain **+3.183% ± 0.399%** (t = 7.98), 28 of 32 seeds positive.

*The finder's own 4-seed table was not sufficient evidence for this* — at 8 seeds t = 2.17, and one
of the four quoted seeds has the opposite sign. Recorded because it is the same error §7.10 names:
the measurement was right, the confidence was not earned until the sample was.

### What this violates

- `sim.json`: *"the multiplier is mean **EXACTLY** 1"* — false, measured 1.013–1.043 by geometry.
- `HarvestWeatherSystem`: *"Sigma means what its name says"* — false, realised sdLog 0.336–0.413.
- **The packet's headline deliverable is defeated.** σ = 0.2936 was DERIVED by inverting
  `CV = √(exp(σ²)−1)` at CV = 0.30 against a reference band of 0.25–0.35. Realised CV is **0.407 in
  the live dev world and 0.431 isolated** — outside the band it was derived from, at every geometry
  the sim actually runs. Replacing a chosen 0.18 with a derived 0.2936 was item 1 of the packet, and
  the derived value is not what the system realises.
- CR-003 §5.2(b) says the multiplier *"acts on OUTPUT, never on the derived 26.0"*. The application
  site is compliant (after the Leontief minimum, never on `landSide`). But a silent uniform +3.2% on
  realised output is observationally identical to running `yieldPerArableKm2PerYear = 26.9`. In
  substance, violated.

### Why it shipped

**No test anywhere measures the multiplier's mean or its realised sigma.** A one-line assertion
`mean(multiplier) ≈ 1` over any populated run would have caught it. See §3.

### Fix (recommended by the verifier, not applied)

Normalise by the true blended variance — two lines inside the existing Pass 3, reusing `sumSq_i`:

```
varInn_i   = 1 + 2·√(k(1−k))/√sumSq_i        // (√k+√(1−k))² on the no-route fallback
innovation = (√k·regional_i + √(1−k)·local_i) / √varInn_i
```

Exact for every geometry including n=1; restores `E[mult]=1` and `sdLog=σ` identically; leaves the
spatial structure, temporal structure, draw order and RNG consumption untouched. **σ needs no
change** — 0.2936 becomes correct once the substrate delivers it.

**Rejected alternative:** excluding self (`w_ii = 0`) makes them independent but fires the degenerate
`sumSq == 0` fallback for any settlement with no reachable neighbour, giving exactly the 1.407×
worst case — and it changes what "regional" means.

### Blast radius, stated plainly

Every quantitative result in T3.4b was measured in the inflated-variance world: the corridor
re-derivation (0.43 → 0.76 %/decade), the six quarantine adjudications, and the migration weight
re-derivation. All need re-measurement. Goldens re-mint. The corridor may move **back toward** band,
since excess variance was inflating gross migration.

## §2 — THE PREMISE THE PACKET WAS COMMISSIONED ON IS FALSE

**The famine mechanism works.** A verifier constructed famine at the *shipped* σ with only store
depth changed, and read real chronicle lines from a mature world — named settlements, real numbers.
The control is clean: at every thin-store calibration the weather-off arm gives **exactly zero**
starvation while weather-on scales monotonically with thinness. Famine emerges from bad years
against thin stores, driven by weather and only by weather. **The mandate's sharpest constraint is
satisfied** — independently confirmed by a full inventory showing no path from the multiplier to
mortality that bypasses the food balance.

**But no unrigged world has thin stores.** 32 worlds, 1000 turns: zero starvation, zero famine
events, max deficit exactly 0.0. Not because weather is weak — the worst realised multiplier is
**0.19**, an 81% harvest failure. Because **grain never spoils, has no cap, and has no alternative
use**, so stores stabilise at **~2,700–3,000 years of consumption** for the whole campaign.

CR-003 §5.2(b) commissioned this packet on the premise of **"thin reserves"**. Measured, the reserves
are **~2,900 years deep — the premise is false by a factor of ~45**, and nothing in the tree records
it. The recorded cause ("the land ceiling is never touched") is also wrong: the world ends its
6,000-year horizon at harvest/demand 2.29 with `landSide/laborSide` 2.13.

**Closing the land frontier is not what stands between this world and famine. Unbounded grain
accumulation is.** That is aimed directly at the M4 plan.

| store depth | famine at shipped σ |
| --- | --- |
| ~2,900 yrs (**shipped**) | none |
| ~180 yrs | first starvation |
| ~65 yrs | mature-world chronicle famines |

Sigma is the wrong lever: **σ ≈ 0.75 (2.6× derived) for any famine at all; σ ≈ 4.0 for famine in the
mature world.** CR-003 §5.1 forbids choosing a constant that reproduces the old crash anyway.

**Consequence for acceptance.** The m3-spec item *"the chronicle again reports famines with named
settlements and real numbers"* is **UNMET in fact and UNMEETABLE inside T3.4b's fixed scope** — the
only routes are a forbidden sigma or a store-bounding mechanism outside the ruling. This needs a
director ruling, not a silent pass. The reporting code itself is proven correct.

### 5.2 "The 12-settlement world is no longer constructible" is false (HIGH — it reached M4 material)

**10/10 seeds place 12 settlements on the production map.** Production capacity: **min 33, median 45,
max 74**. Not nine.

The spacing rule is identical on both maps — 480 km is exactly 30 cost units on each. The cause is
that **the dev preset is not a scale model of the shipped world**: `SizePx = 256` holds `kmPerPx` at
4.0 while noise frequencies stay in normalised coordinates, compressing the continent into ¼ the
linear extent. Land movement cost is 37% higher, so 30 cost units buys ~166 km of separation on dev
against ~450 km on production.

Provenance: a **reasoning error that hardened into a documentation error**. The measurement sentence
in `429f33c` says "measured on the dev world"; the claim sentence drops the qualifier; the test
doc-comment is fully unscoped; by `59180c5` it was promoted to `docs/m4-blocking-material.md` B-1
with an unscoped title. The inference was never re-tested against the shipped map.

**B-1 does not survive as written** — "there is nowhere to put the tenth" is false; there is room for
21 to 62 more. Two residues survive:

1. Saturation is real at **~33**, not 9. That is the number B-1 must state if it states anything.
2. **The larger item B-1 should have raised: 46 call sites across 27 test files calibrate on
