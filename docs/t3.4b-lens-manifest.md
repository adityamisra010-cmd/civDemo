# T3.4b — required-lens manifest

Written **before** implementation, per ADR-015 §7.3 (standing practice: the manifest is a
committed file on every adversarial-mandatory packet, written ahead of a line of code). At
handback each row is checked against the lenses that actually RETURNED RESULTS. **A missing lens
is a BLOCKING condition.**

T3.4b is a **directed** packet whose scope is FIXED by the CR-003 ruling §3 and may not be
widened. It introduces the first stochastic driver into the sim, which is a determinism surface
the project has not had before — hence a lens dedicated to RNG discipline.

| # | lens | what it attacks | required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `rng-determinism` | The new stochastic surface. Same seed → same weather, forever. Streams drawn from `RngRegistry` with state in `WorldState`; no draw order dependent on iteration order that could change; replay and cross-process equality with weather live; a draw added or reordered must break a test. | YES |
| 2 | `correlation-structure` | The two REQUIRED correlations. Spatial: do neighbouring settlements genuinely share weather, and is the sharing strong enough that trade/migration cannot average the risk away? Temporal: are multi-year droughts actually reachable, and does the stated autocorrelation structure produce them at the stated frequency? Attack both with measured distributions, not inspection. | YES |
| 3 | `famine-emergence` | The mandate's sharpest constraint: famine must EMERGE from bad years against thin stores. Hunt for any trigger, threshold, event or special case that fires independently of the food balance. A famine that can occur with full granaries, or fails to occur with empty ones, is the defect. | YES |
| 4 | `quarantine-reconciliation` | The six CR-003-quarantined families — reconciliation, MalthusLite, chronicle famine lines, resurrection detector, migration teeth, artisan recession valve. For EACH: un-quarantined with teeth restored and anchors re-measured, or a precise statement of why it legitimately remains quarantined. **Silence on any of the six is a finding.** | YES |
| 5 | `migration-rederivation` | The weights must be DERIVED from a stated position on what food-per-capita and land-per-capita SHOULD contribute, not rescaled to reproduce old behaviour. Attack the derivation for circularity (a weight justified by the outcome it produces is the cancellation-identity trap again). Check the evidence baseline in `docs/t3.4b-migration-evidence.md` is met: magnitude, convergence, and no single settlement absorbing all activity. | YES |
| 6 | `test-power` | Mutant survival across the packet, including the weather draw, the correlation kernels, and the new corridor test. Named separately per the T3.3/T3.4 precedent. | YES |

## Mutation testing — bounds stated before the sweep (ADR-015 §7.1)

- Clean-suite baseline measured immediately before the sweep and recorded.
- Per-mutant bound **5× the filtered-subset baseline**, hard timeout, killed at the bound.
- A mutant exceeding the bound is recorded `hangs; non-termination under this mutation`.
- One worktree per verifying agent, never shared (ADR-015 §6).

## Guard-test discipline (ADR-015 §7.4)

Every guard this packet ships — the famine-emergence constraint, the migration share corridor,
any clamp on the weather multiplier — ships with a test **proven red** by removing the guard and
watching it fail. The corridor test specifically must fail against a configuration reproducing
the recorded pre-fix migration profile.

## Vacuity discipline (ADR-015 §7.5)

No assertion on a quantity resting against its own limit. Weather multipliers, migration shares
and food stocks all have bounds; before comparing two such values, assert they are strictly
inside those bounds.

## Handback checklist

- [ ] All six lenses returned results (by name, raw/confirmed counts)
- [ ] Any lens that did not report is named BLOCKING
- [ ] Mutant kill-record complete with baseline and bound stated
- [ ] All six quarantined families explicitly addressed — none silent
- [ ] Post-correction migration profile reported against the recorded baseline
- [ ] Every confirmed finding applied or refuted **after** its verdict returned (ADR-015 §6)
