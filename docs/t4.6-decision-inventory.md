## 0. THE STRUCTURAL FINDING THAT PRECEDES EVERY QUESTION BELOW

**There are no polities in any running world, so "foreign" currently has no mechanical referent.**

T4.3 shipped `ClaimRow`, `ControlRow` and `RecognitionRow` (`WorldState.cs:548/567/628`) as
**schema only**. Verified at `8586863`: `grep -rn "next.Claims|next.Controls|next.Recognitions"`
over `Sim.Core/` returns **nothing** — no system writes any of the three, so every world runs with
all three tables empty. T4.5's `AppropriationSystem` documents the same fact from the other side
("nothing in Sim.Core writes Controls … statelessness ALONE is currently true of every settlement").

A foreign exchange is, minimally, an exchange between parties belonging to **different** polities.
With zero polities instantiated, the discriminator that separates domestic from foreign trade
evaluates the same way for every settlement pair in the world. **T4.6's dependency on T4.3 is
satisfied as SCHEMA and unsatisfied as MECHANISM**, and no amount of specification detail closes
that gap — it is closed by whatever packet first writes a `ControlRow`.

Where polity origination is supposed to come from (`docs/d037-emergent-polities.md`): **B1
colonization from below (M4)** — which is **T4.4, currently on hold**; **B2 secession (M5)**; **B3
non-state peoples (M4 worldgen)** — whose mechanism T4.5 shipped but whose *placement* is queued and
undone, so no herding-dominant settlement exists in any founded world either.

**Consequence for sequencing, stated plainly and not as a recommendation:** on the current tree,
T4.6 implemented today could only be exercised by tests that construct polities by hand, and would
move nothing in any world worldgen builds — the same dormancy T4.5 shipped with and recorded. That
is a director-level sequencing question, not a design detail.

## 1. WHAT ALREADY EXISTS, so the inventory does not re-ask settled questions

D-034 trade (`TradeArbitrageSystem`, T3.6) is live and its decisions are already ratified:

| already decided | where | value |
|---|---|---|
| exchange trigger | D-034 / `TradeArbitrageSystem` | per connected settlement pair, per good, once per turn: goods flow when the price gap exceeds transport cost |
| transport cost role | T3.6 decision (a) | a **deadband on the price gap**, never a consumed quantity — nothing is sunk, the only Ledger verb is `Transfer` |
| timing | T3.6 decision (b) | **instantaneous within the turn**; reads PREV, transfers within the step |
| ordering | T3.6 decision (c) | two sweeps, compute-from-PREV then apply; ordering is not load-bearing by construction |
| no global solve | D-034, directed | no cross-pair coupling, no iteration to convergence; a second pass that re-reads its own effects is "the banned shape and a CR, not an optimization" |
| distances | T2.5 `SettlementDistances` | lattice + built paths; unreachable pairs (`PositiveInfinity`) move exactly zero forever |
| pricing | `PriceSystem`, ADR-016 | exact price integration |
| the shared stock | `SystemCatalog` | `GoodStocks` is the sanctioned shared stock; trade is its third holder |
| observable | `TradeFlowRow` | trade's own per-turn table |

**The open question is therefore narrow and specific: what does a POLITY BOUNDARY change about the
above?** Every question below is a form of that question.

## 2. THE DECISIONS REQUIRED

Each row: the question, what the tree already says, and the verdict. **UNRESOLVED** means no
ratified answer exists and the director (or a design conversation like the one T4.7 was granted)
must supply one.

### 2.1 What constitutes a foreign exchange
- **Ratified:** nothing. D-034 knows only "connected settlement pair"; it has no concept of sides.
- **Available discriminator:** `ControlRow(Polity, Place, Strength)` — the polity controlling each
  endpoint. Two endpoints with different controlling polities is the obvious candidate.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether a pair with one *uncontrolled* endpoint (the current universal case, and
  D-037 B3's non-state peoples) is foreign, domestic, or neither. Also whether "foreign" is a
  property of the PAIR or of each SIDE independently (they differ once recognition is asymmetric —
  `RecognitionRow` is keyed `(Recogniser, Recognised)` precisely so A may recognise B without
  reciprocity).

### 2.2 Participants and eligibility
- **Ratified:** trade today is settlement-to-settlement; there is no merchant actor (T4.11 is the
  class that emerges *on trade volume*, so it cannot be a precondition for trade).
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether the trading party is the settlement, the controlling polity, or a class
  within the settlement; whether an uncontrolled settlement may trade foreign at all; whether
  `ClaimRow` without `ControlRow` confers any standing.

### 2.3 Trade initiation
- **Ratified, and load-bearing:** D-034's trigger is **mechanical and unilateral** — a price gap
  exceeding a deadband, evaluated per pair per turn. Nobody decides to trade.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether foreign trade keeps that shape (price-gap-driven, no consent) or requires
  an act by a party. Any consent model introduces an actor with intent, which is AI (M5) and is
  fenced by `docs/m5-ai-constitution.md` — that document is documentary and rules nothing here.

### 2.4 Goods and resources covered
- **Ratified:** D-031 goods, per-good arbitrage over `GoodStocks`.
- **Partially ratified:** capital stocks are NOT goods and do not trade — housing's precedent
  (`Dwellings` never sits in `GoodStocks`), which `docs/t4.13-design-record.md` follows for
  household goods.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether any good is non-exportable across a polity boundary (strategic goods,
  food under famine), which is a policy verb nothing in the tree has.

### 2.5 Exchange valuation and pricing
- **Ratified:** `PriceSystem` + ADR-016 exact price integration; the gap-versus-deadband test.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether a polity boundary alters the deadband (a tariff/friction term) or the
  price signal itself. **A tariff is the single most likely place this packet would invent a
  constant**, and CLAUDE.md law 2 plus CR-003's unfalsifiable-constant bar both bite: any such
  coefficient needs a derivation and a falsifiable measurement, not a chosen number.

### 2.6 Transport and logistics
- **Ratified and recently changed:** T2.5 `SettlementDistances` over lattice + built paths, now
  river-aware via T4.7's `riverCostFactor`. Transport enters as a **deadband**, never as a transit
  loss (T3.6 decision (a)).
- **UNRESOLVED:** nothing structural — foreign trade should inherit this unchanged unless a boundary
  is itself a distance/cost term, which is 2.5's question.

### 2.7 Settlement and ownership
- **Ratified:** `Ledger.Transfer` moves goods between two settlements' stock rows and conserves by
  construction; there is no ownership concept beyond which settlement's row holds the units.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether goods in transit exist (today they do not — trade is instantaneous), and
  whether a polity owns anything distinct from its settlements' stocks. **A polity-level treasury or
  stockpile would be a new conserved carrier and a schema change.**

### 2.8 Diplomatic prerequisites
- **Ratified:** `RecognitionRow(Recogniser, Recognised)` exists as schema; **nothing writes it** and
  no system reads it.
- **UNRESOLVED, entirely.** Whether trade requires recognition, whether non-recognition blocks or
  merely prices, and what a war/hostility state would do — D-037's conflict ladder is M5/M8 and
  T4.8's battle contract is deferred to M6. There is no diplomacy mechanism in the tree to gate on.

### 2.9 Failure and cancellation
- **Ratified by construction:** today a trade that cannot happen simply does not — unreachable pairs
  move zero, and `ClampToAvailable` bounds what a stock can give.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether foreign trade has a failure MODE distinct from not-happening (embargo,
  seizure, default). Each such mode is a new mechanism, and per D-037 A1 nothing may spawn.

### 2.10 Information available to player and AI
- **Ratified as principle:** Spine principle 7 **Symmetry** (`civ-sim-architecture-v3-outline.md:25`)
  — *"AI actors use player-identical verbs and information class. Difficulty = information and
  friction, never hidden resources."* D-039 (command friction, reconnaissance) and D-040 (discovery
  and control) own information boundaries.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether a polity can see a foreign settlement's prices and stocks at all. This is
  **not cosmetic**: D-034's mechanism reads the counterparty's PREV price to compute the gap, so if
  foreign prices are not observable the existing trigger cannot be reused across a boundary. This is
  the deepest coupling in the inventory and it is unowned.

### 2.11 Immediate versus accumulated timing
- **Ratified:** instantaneous within the turn (T3.6 decision (b)), with the dt at which that breaks
  stated in the T3.6 spec's forward note.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether distance across a polity boundary is long enough to need in-transit state.
  If it is, that is a new serialized row and a schema change, and it reopens T3.6 decision (b).

### 2.12 Interaction with territory and settlement claims
- **UNRESOLVED.** Whether trade is affected by `ClaimRow` overlap (contested territory), whether
  routes crossing a third polity's controlled settlements incur anything, and whether trade itself
  changes claim strength. Note D-040 owns discovery/control; nothing links trade to claims today.

### 2.13 Interaction with money — **and the deferral that governs it**
- **Ratified and binding:** **money does not exist and is deferred.** D-030: *"Money as an
  institution is deferred to its own milestone"*; the M3 scope fence repeats it; and
  `docs/m4-pre-spec-dependencies.md`:25-31 (GOV-2) records the gap that **no money milestone exists
  anywhere** in the ladder.
- **Therefore, not a question but a constraint:** foreign trade at M4 is **barter — good against
  good**, exactly as D-034 already is. Any balance-of-payments, credit, tribute or price-in-silver
  concept is out of scope by ratified deferral. T4.8's R-1 ruling set the same fence for notables
  ("purchase is out of scope — payment is money, M5").
- **UNRESOLVED (for the director, not for T4.6):** GOV-2's finding that the money milestone is
  missing from the ladder remains open and is a prerequisite for any later foreign-trade economics.

### 2.14 Conservation requirements
- **Ratified, non-negotiable (CLAUDE.md law 1):** every unit moves via `Ledger.Transfer`/`Flow`;
  conserved stocks are `long`; tests assert exact equality with no epsilon. `Transfer` conserves by
  construction and takes no `ReasonId`; a sink-plus-source pair under a new reason would satisfy
  "a dedicated reason" while **breaking** exact conservation (the T4.5 finding).
- **Consequence:** any foreign-trade loss, tariff-in-kind or seizure needs a **carrier and a named
  sink reason**, or it is a store drain in disguise — the backdoor T3.6 decision (a) already closed.

### 2.15 Determinism requirements
- **Ratified, non-negotiable (law 5):** no `System.Random`, no `Dictionary`/`HashSet` iteration in
  sim logic, no `GetHashCode` as logic input, no `float`, no culture-sensitive formatting, no LINQ in
  hot paths; all randomness through `RngRegistry` with state in `WorldState`.
- **Ratified, and the one most likely to be violated here:** every ordering or argmax over
  double-valued scores uses a composite key with a **stable integer tie-break `(score, id)`** and
  **ships a tie-dense test proving it**. Foreign trade adds at least one new ordering (which
  counterparty, which polity pair). **T4.5's shipped defect was exactly this** — an argmax that
  claimed the composite key and implemented first-row-scanned, with a tie test built in ascending
  order so it could never see the difference.
- **Ratified:** trade's two-sweep shape exists so ordering is not load-bearing; any foreign extension
  must preserve that or justify departing from it.

### 2.16 Provenance for every future constant
- **Ratified and strict.** CLAUDE.md: mechanisms over modifiers; coefficients inside resolution
  equations are fine, free-floating permanent buffs are banned; rates are per-sim-year integrated
  with `dtYears`; "DERIVE, DO NOT FIT — out-of-band with a derived constant beats in-band with a
  fitted one" (T3.2b item 1, quoted in CR-003 §1).
- **The bar, from CR-003 and T4.10:** a constant that cannot be *falsified* by measurement must not
  ship. T4.10's food-term removal is the precedent — the replacement signal measured identically
  1.0 across 4 seeds × 3 checkpoints × 12 settlements, so no coefficient for it could be derived,
  validated or refuted, and the term was **removed rather than guessed**. T4.12 is the other
  precedent: `BaseRatePerYear` 0.03 was audited to OUTCOME C — semantically understood, numerically
  underived — and retained explicitly as a historical TUNE rather than dressed up as derived.
- **Applies to:** any tariff, any boundary friction, any foreign-trade elasticity, any recognition
  threshold. Each needs a reference class stated **before** the number is computed (the T4.7
  `riverCostFactor` pattern: band from Diocletian's Price Edict and medieval cartage rates *first*,
  conservative edge chosen *second*).

### 2.17 Golden and test implications
- **Certain:** all three behavioural goldens (`DrivenGolden`, `FoundedGolden`/`ci.yml`'s
  `FOUNDED_GOLDEN`, `FirstReign`) move if any world's flows change. `GoldenHash_Seed42Turn200`
  (synthetic `Genesis(23)`, no terrain) is the no-unrelated-movement control and should NOT move.
- **Required discipline:** re-derive by measurement on the tree under test, never carry a value
  through a rebase; record **OLD → NEW → CAUSE** at every pin including `ci.yml`.
- **Required if any new row type ships:** a POPULATED-table test with exact `ExpectedLength`,
  bit-exact round-trip and hash equality; empty-table coverage proves nothing (T1.1/T1.3 precedent).
  Schema version bumps with the three edits `CanonicalSchema`'s own contract names.
- **Required, adversarial-mandatory:** the packet row says **YES**. Under ADR-015 that means one
  worktree per verifying agent pinned to the packet commit, mutants killed by **semantic** tests
  (golden-only kills do not count), and no finding actioned before its verdict returns.
- **Corridor watch:** `canonical.migrationGrossPerDecade` is currently **out of band on 19 of 20
  seeds** and under active bisection by T4.10; trade changes reach migration through settlement
  attractiveness, so T4.6 must not be measured against corridors while that is unresolved.
