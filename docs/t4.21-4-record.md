### 2.1 Instrument

`TestConfigs.Worldgen()`, the shipped pipeline and era table), classifying every settlement on the

### 2.2 (a) Famine frequency — canonical, 20 seeds × 300 founded turns, λ = 0.01

| quantity | measured |

**Against λ·100 = 1.0.** The once-per-turn booking truncation at dt 10 is exact and derivable:
`P(onset in a turn) = 1 − e^{−λ·dt} = 0.0951626` against an expected count of `λ·dt = 0.1`, i.e.
a truncation factor of **0.9516258** (a 4.84 % loss — the `_doc`'s stated "≈ 4.7 %"). The
truncation-corrected expectation is therefore 0.9516258 onsets per settlement-century. The
binomial 99 % band on 66,000 trials at p = 0.0951626 is **[0.922204, 0.981047]** per
settlement-century; the measured 0.941061 lies inside it at **z = −0.925**. Against the raw
λ·100 = 1.0 the measurement is 5.89 % low, of which 4.84 pp is the stated truncation.

**Starvation.** 11,060 starvation deaths at seed 42 alone over 300 turns; across all 20 seeds 194,896 of the
194,919 starvation deaths fell on a turn whose PREV world contained at least one FAMINE settlement.
The 23 exceptions (seed 16) fell on the single turn whose highest class was SEVERE — the ladder's
SEVERE arm firing exactly as specified.

### 4.2 RULE B — the dev migration quarantine's recorded envelope

Re-pinned to the MEASURED values, cause named, `DriftTolerance` unchanged at 0.75, band untouched:

| seed | old recorded | new recorded | ratio | λ = 0 twin, same run |

The cause is split and both halves are measured: bounded flight + basin caps + vacancy bound
(T4.21-2) put seed 7 at 1.02E-04 — which is what made its drift tooth the ONE inherited red on
`8f7f9da` — and the ARMING is the other half and dominates.

**Deviation from RULE B's letter, stated.** RULE B assumed the drift tooth (a LOWER bound) was the
live one. It is not: the value went UP by 19–206×, and the tooth that fires is the T3.4c RESOLUTION
tooth `value < lo`, whose message says *"back INSIDE the corridor … RESOLVED"*. That message is now
FALSE — the value is 1.5× ABOVE the ceiling, not inside the band. Writing a false statement into a
failure message is the exact T3.4b defect this helper exists to have fixed, so the tooth was SPLIT:
"inside [lo, hi]" keeps its own loud resolution tooth, and "above hi" is held by the recorded
envelope's own tolerance in both directions. Teeth were ADDED, none removed, no band read is
changed, and 0.75 stays 0.75.

**NOT EXECUTED ON THIS TREE — stated so it is not read as an observed result.** The re-pinned
envelope and the new teeth do not run on any test today: both call-site seeds fail EARLIER, in
`AssertMalthusKnownDeviation` (`CalibrationBatteryTests.cs:624`, the `starvedTotal == 0` tooth,
reached from `:557`), and `AssertDevMigrationQuarantine` is only called on the line after. Measured
on `ddeba85`: seed 42 fails with 5,983 starvation deaths, seed 7 with 6,414, both at `:624`. The
values in the table above were therefore measured DIRECTLY from the dev runs, not through the test,
and the teeth take effect only once the CR-016 Malthus disposition is ruled.

**T4.21-6 FOLLOW-UP — the split shipped with its resolution tooth UNREACHABLE.** Because nothing
executed it, the ordering defect was not visible here: the downward drift tooth was evaluated
FIRST, and with the re-pinned values its bar (recorded × 0.75 = 0.01113071385 for seed 42,
0.011887221825 for seed 7) sits ABOVE the corridor ceiling `hi = 0.01`. The envelope bracket and
the band no longer overlap, so every value inside `[lo, hi]` — the RESOLUTION this quarantine exists
to detect — failed the drift tooth first and was reported as *"degraded … A NEW defect"*, while the
resolution tooth below had an empty failure set. Fixed on `ddeba85`: the band read comes first, the
envelope teeth judge only the above-ceiling regime, and
`T4216_DevMigrationQuarantine_EachToothAnswersItsOwnRegime` now drives the helper directly with
synthetic readings so all four teeth — and the non-overlap premise the ordering rests on — are
exercised without a world run.
