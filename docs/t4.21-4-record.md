## §2. What arming did — measured

### 2.1 Instrument

A temporary in-tree harness stepped the canonical founded world (`WorldFounding.Found`,
`TestConfigs.Worldgen()`, the shipped pipeline and era table), classifying every settlement on the
world the demographic kernel READ (PREV) with `FoodState.Of`, counting disaster ONSETS on the world
it WROTE, and reading the global starvation sink from `LedgerFlows`. An onset is a row with
`Kind = 1` whose previous-turn row did not have `RemainingYears > 0` — exactly the
"no new onset while active" branch of `DisasterSystem.Step`.

### 2.2 (a) Famine frequency — canonical, 20 seeds × 300 founded turns, λ = 0.01

| quantity | measured |
| --- | --- |
| settlement-turns | 66,000 (660,000 settlement-years at dt 10) |
| disaster onsets | 6,211 |
| onsets per settlement-century | **0.941061** |
| FAMINE settlement-turns | 1,540 (2.33 % of settlement-turns) |
| — of which `FamineReason.Disaster` | 1,540 |
| — of which `FamineReason.Abandonment` / `Both` / **`None`** | 0 / 0 / **0** |
| SEVERE settlement-turns | 2 |
| STRESS settlement-turns | 263 |
| NORMAL settlement-turns | 70,195 |

**Against λ·100 = 1.0.** The once-per-turn booking truncation at dt 10 is exact and derivable:
`P(onset in a turn) = 1 − e^{−λ·dt} = 0.0951626` against an expected count of `λ·dt = 0.1`, i.e.
a truncation factor of **0.9516258** (a 4.84 % loss — the `_doc`'s stated "≈ 4.7 %"). The
truncation-corrected expectation is therefore 0.9516258 onsets per settlement-century. The
binomial 99 % band on 66,000 trials at p = 0.0951626 is **[0.922204, 0.981047]** per
settlement-century; the measured 0.941061 lies inside it at **z = −0.925**. Against the raw
λ·100 = 1.0 the measurement is 5.89 % low, of which 4.84 pp is the stated truncation.

**Starvation.** 11,060 starvation deaths at seed 42 alone over 300 turns; across all 20 seeds 194,896 of the
194,919 starvation deaths fell on a turn whose PREV world contained at least one FAMINE settlement.
The 23 exceptions (seed 16) fell on the single turn whose highest class was SEVERE — the ladder's
SEVERE arm firing exactly as specified.

### 2.3 (b) V1 — every FAMINE settlement-turn has a cause

`FamineReason.None` count over 1,540 FAMINE settlement-turns (20 seeds) and 78 (seed 42):

### 2.4 (c) V2 — zero starvation in STRESS

The ledger's starvation sink is global, so the per-settlement attribution available from the
shipped state is a CLASS PARTITION: for each turn, the highest `FoodState` over the PREV world.
Measured over 20 seeds × 300 turns:

| highest class on PREV | turns | starvation deaths on the following step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FAMINE | 1,254 | 194,896 |
| SEVERE | 1 | 23 |
| STRESS | 158 | **0** |
| NORMAL | 4,587 | **0** |

**Zero violations.** No turn on which every settlement was NORMAL or STRESS produced a single
starvation death, in either arm. The remaining STRESS settlement-turns (263 − 158 co-occurring with
a FAMINE elsewhere in the world) are covered by construction and by the scenario battery's
per-settlement `d_eff` assertion, not by this global partition; that limit of the instrument is
stated rather than papered over.
