# T3.5b — the two reference-class derivations

**Both derivations are committed BEFORE any measurement of what they do to grievance, satisfaction,
corridors or goldens** (lens 1's commit-order requirement). The discipline is T3.2b's: state the
reference class, fix the numbers from it, record the justification, then measure.

## 1. The founding default sector allocation

### Reference class

**The mixed-farming river-valley village of the late Neolithic / early Bronze Age** — which is what
the sim's own worldgen makes every settlement: ADR-013 established that an f = 1.0 km² is
river-valley floor within 8 km of a channel, sites are chosen riverine, and the founding population
is a few hundred people working cereals with hand tools, keeping a traction/manure herd, fishing a
live river, building in timber and daub, and making their own pots, tools and cloth.

The labour-year of such a village, from the ethnographic and archaeological time-budget literature
on subsistence cereal agriculturalists (cross-cultural time-allocation studies; the experimental-
archaeology labour accounting for Neolithic building and craft; the textile-labour literature that
consistently finds spinning and weaving to be the great underestimated draw on pre-modern hours):

| activity | share of productive labour-year | reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **cultivation** | **0.55** | The majority draw, but not a monoculture of effort: field prep, sowing, weeding, harvest, threshing, storage. Time-allocation studies of hoe/ard cereal farmers put cultivation at 50–60% of productive hours across the year (strongly seasonal — harvest peaks near total, winter near zero — but the ALLOCATION row is a year-round mean). Below 0.5 is a society that has left subsistence farming; above ~0.65 is a monocrop caricature no ethnography supports. |
| **herding + fishing** | **0.15** | The sim's settlements are riverine BY CONSTRUCTION and ADR-013's own land budget reserves 25 ha per 100 for the traction-and-manure herd — a herd that must be watched, watered, folded and milked year-round. Riverine fishing is low-capital, high-return protein. Mixed-farming villages run 12–18% here; pure pastoralists far more, but this reference class is the mixed farmer. |
| **extraction** | **0.10** | Fuel wood is the silent constant: a household fire burns daily, and woodcutting-plus-hauling for fuel, building timber, clay and stone is a steady ~8–12% of the labour-year in temperate-forest agricultural communities. |
| **crafting** | **0.12** | Pottery, tool haft and blade replacement, hide-working — and above all TEXTILES: spinning alone, at pre-wheel speeds, consumes hundreds of hours per garment-set per year. The textile literature is unambiguous that craft in a self-provisioning village is second only to cultivation; 10–15% is the defensible band, and the sim's tools-are-real-goods loop (T3.3) needs this labour to exist at all. |
| **construction** | **0.08** | Dwelling repair, granary building, and the path work PathBuild models. Experimental archaeology prices a timber longhouse at a few thousand person-hours — amortised over a decade-plus life, plus annual re-thatching/re-daubing, this is 5–10% of the village year. |

**Sum = 1.00.** Fixed here, before measurement.

### Why these and not others (anti-fitting statement)

The numbers were chosen from the activity reasoning above, written down, and only then measured.
No corridor, grievance, satisfaction or golden value was computed with any candidate mix before
this file was committed — the commit order is the proof (lens 1's brief). The mix was NOT chosen
to sit above s\* (the pre-committed threshold analysis was filed one commit earlier and gives
s\* = 0.295 worst-case; a 0.55 farming share was never in tension with it, and had the reference
class produced 0.25 the pre-committed reading — report the regime change, do not raise the mix —
would have governed).

### Where the values live

`Sectors.Default` (`Sim.Core/State/WorldState.cs`) — deliberately code, not data: the sector
registry is kernel-contract surface (D-032), and the default is the never-ordered founding state,
not a tuning surface. The values carry this file as their provenance.

## 2. The variety reference point — a fixed nutritional diversity standard

### The ruled-out candidates, and why (recorded for the record)

- **Perfect-evenness normalisation** (the shipped form): concentration is measured against 1/n.
  No real diet is even across its goods — a staple-based diet is 70–80% staple by construction —
  so a fully supplied world grieves forever and exact saturation is unreachable (measured DEAD at
  T3.5 review: peasant Sustenance capped at 0.8916).
- **Declared-basket normalisation:** monotony measured against what the class asked for lets a
  settlement self-certify out of the penalty by declaring monotony. Authoring becomes load-bearing.

### Reference class

**The varied pre-modern agrarian diet** — what nutrition history actually reconstructs for a
well-supplied (not famine, not elite) cereal-based population: cereals/staple ~65–75% of calories,
animal products (meat, dairy, eggs, fish) ~10–20%, and the remainder legumes, vegetables, fruit,
oil and drink. ADR-013's own yield chain already commits this world to "cereals 75% of calories" as
its baseline diet; a VARIED version of that diet — the thing D-035-A rewards — sits at the
diverse end of the reconstructed range.

**The standard, fixed here:** staple **0.70**, animal/fish **0.20**, other **0.10** of Sustenance
calories. Its Herfindahl concentration:

    H* = 0.70² + 0.20² + 0.10² = 0.49 + 0.04 + 0.01 = **0.54**

### The mechanism

The variety factor measures EXCESS concentration beyond the standard, not distance from
perfect evenness:

    excess = max(0, (H_obtained − H*) / (1 − H*))
    factor = 1 − varietyWeight × excess

- **A diet at or below H\* takes no penalty** — factor exactly 1.0, so full satisfaction is
  REACHABLE and the exact-saturation branch comes back to life (§7.4 obligation: proven live).
- **A grain-only diet** (H = 1) takes the full penalty: factor = 1 − varietyWeight — strictly
  below any fish-importing diet. D-035-A's reason-to-trade survives, and is MEASURED, not asserted.
- The standard is independent of both the declared basket and what was received — the two failure
  modes of the ruled-out candidates.
- H\* lives in DATA (`needs.json`, as the three reference shares, with H\* computed at load) so it
  is tunable and auditable; the anti-tautology test perturbs it and asserts satisfaction responds.

### Anti-fitting statement

The standard derives from the diet-history reference class and from ADR-013's own "cereals 75% of
calories" commitment — not from the shipped baskets (peasant 0.90/0.06/0.04 has H = 0.8152, which
under this standard takes a penalty: the shipped world is measurably LESS varied than a good
pre-modern diet, which is the honest reading of a frontier grain economy). No satisfaction value
was computed with any candidate standard before this file was committed.
