## §12 GAPS — SUBJECTS IN THIS CLUSTER ON WHICH THE REPOSITORY RECORDS NO DECISION

Stated as questions for the Director, per the mandate. MEASURED: each was searched for across
`docs/`, `Sim.Core/`, `Sim.Data/` at HEAD and no decision document, ADR, CR, ruling, code comment or
`_doc` string was found. Absence of a decision is not a proposal.

**Climate and weather**

1. **Is there to be a CLIMATE at all, distinct from weather?** The tree has a static temperature and
   moisture field (E-16, E-17) and a stochastic output multiplier (E-29), and nothing between them.
   No document records a decision either way.
2. **Is seasonality in scope?** `grep -rn -iE "ENSO|monsoon|seasonal"` over `docs/` returns no
   decision — only `docs/d035-needs-aggregation.md:91` using "a season" as an example of a physical
   carrier, and the T3.4b migration pathology *"40 % in one season"*. At a canonical dt of 10
   sim-years a season is not representable; no document says whether that is permanent.
3. **Should weather affect anything other than food output?** Today it multiplies exactly two food
   paths and nothing else (E-35, E-99). No decision records whether construction, travel, health,
   mortality or trade should see weather.
4. **Is weather to remain SETTLEMENT-scoped, or become a FIELD over the map?** The shipped model has
   one AR(1) state per settlement, correlated through travel costs (E-32). Nothing records whether a
   map-scale field was considered and rejected, or simply never raised.
5. **At what dt is weather meant to be meaningful?** τ = 3 y against a canonical dt of 10 y makes the
   AR(1) memory inert in the Neolithic band (E-38), and G1 says the decade multiplier is 1.5× too
   variable (E-45). No document states what the weather process is supposed to MEAN at a decade
   resolution.

**Soil, land quality and land use**

6. **Is soil to be modelled separately from fertility?** D-009/D-010 name soil as an intended raster
   layer (E-08); no soil layer exists (E-14); D-022 anti-scopes erosion (E-11). No document
   reconciles the three.
7. **Can land quality CHANGE?** Fertility has no writer and terrain is immutable (E-13). Nothing
   records whether soil depletion, manuring, terracing, salinisation or fallow quality are intended,
   deferred or refused — only that *erosion* is anti-scoped at M1 worldgen fidelity.
8. **Is land USE to be represented at all?** ADR-013's derivation assumes a land budget inside each
   ideal km² — cropped share 0.28, fallow rotation 2 years in 7, pasture 25 ha, woodland 15 ha
   (`adr-013:225-226`) — but **none of it is state**: it is arithmetic inside a constant. No decision
   records whether that budget should ever become a modelled quantity.
9. **Is there to be a deforestation or vegetation mechanism?** D-009/D-010 name vegetation as a layer
   (E-08); nothing else in the tree mentions it.

**Water**

10. **Is irrigation in scope?** MEASURED: `grep -rn -i "irrigat"` over `docs/`, `Sim.Core/` and
    `Sim.Data/` returns **only** the phrase *"rain-fed cereal agriculture without irrigation or
    modern inputs"* as the σ and yield reference class (`SimConfig.cs:371`; `sim.json:223`;
    `docs/t3.4b-migration-evidence.md:149`). **Irrigation is the reference class's exclusion, not a
    recorded decision about the game.** Was it refused, deferred, or never asked?
11. **Is water a constraint on agriculture, or only an input to fertility?** Moisture enters yield
    once, at worldgen, through a distance proxy (E-17). No decision records whether water availability
    should bind production, or vary.
12. **Is water transport ever to exist as a MEDIUM rather than a cost coefficient?** E-87 rules
    vessels, ports and naval movement out of scope for T4.7; Q-A (E-85) stands OPEN with three named
    architectural options. No document says which of those is the intended end state, or whether
    D-040 B3's "boats" (`docs/queue.md:546`) settles it.
13. **Are floods in scope?** The disaster registry has exactly one kind, crop failure (E-99). Flood,
    fire, storm, murrain, blight and pest have a named extension slot (E-110) and no decision.

**Pollution and environmental degradation**

14. **What is a "degradation stock"?** The Spine's M9 row names *"degradation stocks close loops"*
    (E-07) and no document elaborates. `grep -rn -i "pollution"` over `docs/`, `Sim.Core/` and
    `Sim.Data/` returns **zero** hits at HEAD. Under law 1 and law 7 a degradation stock would be a
    conserved `long` moving only through the Ledger — is that the intent?
15. **Does pollution/degradation belong to the terrain rasters (needing the ADR-008 reversal, E-15)
    or to a per-settlement table?** Nothing records the choice, and the two have very different
    costs.
16. **Is the M9 placement of environment & climate still the intent?** The only source is the Spine
    system table (E-07), written before M0. Nothing since re-states it.

**Cross-cutting**

17. **Who owns the FOURTH PATH?** See E-96 below. No packet, milestone or owner is recorded for the
    relayed direction, and CR-015's three G1 options do not describe it.
18. **What replaces `outputPerFarmerPerYear` = 5.0?** The labour side of the agronomic `min()` is
    CHOSEN, never derived (E-58), while the land side carries a full derivation. No packet owns
    deriving it, and E-57 shows the binding side depends on it.
