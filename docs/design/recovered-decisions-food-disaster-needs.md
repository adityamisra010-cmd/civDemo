## §0 THE THREE DISTINCTIONS THAT MUST BE MADE FIRST

RATIFIED. Three pairs are routinely collapsed by readers of this cluster, and collapsing any of them
invalidates the rest of the record.

**(1) HAPPINESS is not the NEEDS AGGREGATE.** They are two different numbers computed from
overlapping inputs by two different pieces of code, deliberately. `SettlementHappiness` is a derived
0..100 reading over Food and Housing that DRIVES BEHAVIOUR (migration, revolt) today; the
`NeedsGrievance` needs aggregate is an eight-need ladder that drives NOTHING until M5 (D-021). The
separation is enforced by a CI gate. *"It is deliberately not the same number as the needs aggregate
that accrues grievance"* (`Sim.Core/Observability/Explain/HappinessExplanation.cs:40-41`).

**(2) The DEFICIT is not a FAMINE.** `ConsumptionDeficitRow.DeficitRatio` is the food balance's
signal in every state. FAMINE is a separate DERIVED label requiring a deficit AND an exceptional
cause. *"The consumption deficit is the food balance's signal and stays so in every state; it is not
a famine"* (`docs/adr/cr-015-famine-is-exceptional.md:371-372`).

**(3) The DISASTER MECHANISM exists but is INERT.** `DisasterSystem`, `DisasterRow` and schema v25
ship complete and tested; `sim.json disaster.hazardPerYear` is **0.0**
(`Sim.Data/content/sim.json:235`). Famine is reachable in play today only through the OTHER cause,
deliberate abandonment. Anything that reads "the disaster fires" is reading T4.21-4's ARMED
measurement, which is not the shipped world.
