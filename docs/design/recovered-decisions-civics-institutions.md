## §5 THE REJECTION RECORD — THE UNIVERSAL `CapabilitySystem`

*The mandate requires this instance be recorded with the precise reason it was rejected and what
would have to change for it to be revisited. Nothing here is a recommendation.*

**WHAT WAS PROPOSED.** `docs/capability-architecture-decision.md` §5 proposed a capability *seam*
shaped `CapabilityState(scope, domain, capability)` — a single place where every domain's
capability answers would be held and evaluated (recorded at
`docs/d042-empire-and-player-control-addendum.md:276-282`). The broader shape the ruling names is
a *"universal God system such as a `CapabilitySystem`* that owns every capability or coordinates
every domain" (`:141-142`).

**THE RULING, verbatim.** *"**Do not create a universal God system such as a `CapabilitySystem`**
that owns every capability or coordinates every domain."*
(`docs/d042-empire-and-player-control-addendum.md:141-142`, §7.3.) It is repeated in the §12
anti-pattern list as *"a universal `CapabilitySystem` God object"* (`:196-203`). — RATIFIED.

**THE PRECISE REASON, as the document gives it.** The rejection sits inside §7, whose title is
*"STATE-MEDIATED DEPENDENCY (reaffirms Law 6)"*, and whose two preceding clauses are the reason:
*"**Gameplay interdependence is allowed; direct code coupling is not.** Systems communicate
through World State and kernel contracts, **never by calling sibling domain systems**"* (§7.1,
`:136-138`), and *"Economy may depend *conceptually* on knowledge, government, military,
transport and institutions — **that never justifies a sibling call**"* (§7.2, `:139-140`).
— RATIFIED. A coordinating capability owner is by construction a system every other system must
call or be called by, which is the Law 6 violation (`CLAUDE.md:21`). A second, independent ground
is §3.2's God-object prohibition applied to the Empire (`:60-62`) — the same structural objection
one level up. — INFERRED (the document places the clause in §7 and repeats it in §12; it does not
separately restate §3.2 as a ground).

**WHAT SURVIVED THE REJECTION.** Not nothing. §8.1 rules *"**The existing D-020 predicate
machinery is the foundation** for future capability evaluation"* (`:146-147`), and §14.5 states
the seam *"is conformant **only** as a *shared predicate grammar consumed independently by each
domain system* — the shape D-020 already has — **and not** as a coordinating owner"*
(`:276-282`). — RATIFIED. So the rejected thing is the OWNERSHIP and COORDINATION, not the shared
grammar.

**BLAST RADIUS OF THE REJECTION, as recorded.**
`docs/d042-empire-and-player-control-addendum.md:8-9` states the record designs no mechanism and
changed no code, test, golden or frozen document, and §14.5 says the correction was *"Recorded in
that document too"* rather than by rewriting it. — RATIFIED. The shipped tree is unaffected: the
two live D-020 consumers (class emergence; recipe `requires`) are independent consumers of the
grammar already (`docs/milestone-architecture-governance.md:96-98`). — MEASURED, cited to that
record on this tree (secondary evidence under GOV-4).

**WHAT WOULD HAVE TO CHANGE FOR IT TO BE REVISITED.** Three things, each stated from a document
rather than invented here:

1. **Law 6 would have to be amended, or a Law-6-conformant coordinator demonstrated.** The
   rejection is filed under a section headed *"reaffirms Law 6"*; Law 6 is frozen
   (`docs/spine-s8-governance-freeze.md:14`, `CLAUDE.md:21`), so reopening needs a Contradiction
   Report plus a director ADR (`docs/spine-s8-governance-freeze.md:37`). — RATIFIED.
2. **§8.3's Empire-scope requirement would have to be satisfied some other way.** D-042 requires
   capability evaluation to distinguish Empire-level and Settlement-level scope (`:155-156`) while
   the shipped `VariableRow` is settlement-keyed only
   (`docs/milestone-architecture-governance.md:101-102`). A universal owner is one way to add
   Empire scope; the ruling forbids it without saying what replaces it. — **DIRECTOR DECISION
   REQUIRED** (this is **C-09**).
3. **The "institution" definition would have to be settled first.** Two of the six recorded
   meanings of *institution* make it a published scalar and one makes it a composable module
   (`docs/milestone-architecture-governance.md:196-198`) — which of those a capability layer
   consumes changes whether a coordinator is even tempting. CR-010 was recommended
   (`docs/milestone-architecture-governance.md:299-300`) and **never written**
   (`docs/current-state.md:13`). — RATIFIED that it is unwritten; OPEN as a decision.

**RECORDED, NOT RECOMMENDED.** This lane takes no position on whether the rejection should be
revisited.
