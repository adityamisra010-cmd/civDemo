## THE MECHANISM AS BUILT

Two sinks in `ConsumptionSystem.BoundStore`, applied **after** eating (people eat before the store
spoils or overflows; the reverse order would starve a settlement for an evaluation-order reason):

1. **SPOILAGE** — `ReasonIds.Spoilage (14)`, `Ledger.Flow` sink on the grain stock.
   **Integrated as a survival exponential `1 − exp(−rate·dt)`, not `rate·dt`** — at the canonical
   dt of 10 years the linear form would destroy 80 % of the store in one turn and 100 % at
   dt = 12.5. **The exponential form is dt-invariant.**
2. **GRANARY CAPACITY** — `ReasonIds.GranaryOverflow (15)`, sink on the excess above
   `1.5 × annual grain demand`. Denominated in years of the settlement's OWN demand, so it scales
   with population and adds no per-world constant.

**Two reasons, not one, deliberately:** "why is the store not growing?" has two different answers,
and merging a decay process with a capacity constraint would destroy the audit trail. Conservation
is exact — stocks stay `long`, both losses go through the Ledger.

**Stated, not hidden:** neither loss banks a sub-unit remainder, so a store whose annual spoilage is
under half a unit does not spoil. A remainder field would change the serialized row shape, which is
outside this packet's fence.

## 1–2. THE TRIGGER, AND ITS VALUE

## 3. SOURCE — PREVIOUS-TURN PUBLISHED STATE, DEFAULTING BECAUSE NOTHING WAS EVER PUBLISHED

`gapPeople = targetShare × totalAdults − managedAdults`. **`targetShare = 0.000000`** because

**Traced one level further, as required:** `surplus` is read from **`prev.Variables`**

defaults to 0.0 — not because the settlement has no surplus, but because NOTHING HAS WRITTEN THE
VARIABLE YET.**

## 4. TRACED ONE LEVEL FURTHER — CONFIRMED, NOT INFERRED

`prev.Variables` is empty at world construction: `ClassWorld` and `PopulationExactnessTests.

would populate `Variables` (that publication is `ClassMobilitySystem`'s own downstream write, which

is surprising is that a hand-built test world beginning mid-simulation (with a large pre-existing
artisan class) triggers the SAME guard a freshly founded world triggers at turn 1, and the guard's
consequence — `surplus = 0` → `targetShare = 0` → **treat the entire existing artisan class as
excess** — is appropriate for a world with no artisans yet and is NOT obviously appropriate for a
world seeded with 4,500 already-active ones.

## 5. VALUES IMMEDIATELY BEFORE CLASSMOBILITY EXECUTES
