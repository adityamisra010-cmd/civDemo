## The three corrections

### 1. The resurrection detector's un-quarantine was credited to the wrong mechanism

`429f33c` and `11a1800` state: *"Harvest variance restored the precondition: settlements now die
food-less."* Measured by mechanism removal (σ → 0, which makes the multiplier exactly 1 — a clean
removal with no other path):

| config | first food-less death | any death? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| shipped (σ = 0.2936, landWeight 0.078125) | turn 12 | yes |
| **σ = 0**, landWeight 0.078125 | **turn 45** | **yes** |
| σ = 0, landWeight **0.00390625** (pre-packet) | **never** | **no** |

**The precondition was restored by the migration `attractivenessLandWeight` re-derivation, not by
harvest variance.** Weather only makes it happen earlier. Worse for the stated mechanism: the
settlement empties by *migration* (end population 0 vs 4 retained under the old weight), which is
depopulation, not the famine death the narrative claims.

The un-quarantine itself is correct and the guard does have teeth. **The recorded reason is wrong**,
and the root cause is visible in the history: weather and the weight change landed in one commit, so
the guard firing was attributed to the headline change without isolating it.

### 2. The artisan quarantine's stated reason is false — measured at HEAD (T3.4c review fix)

`ClassSystemTests` states: *"surplus stays above the targetShare saturation point (2.0) for the whole
campaign, the share pins at the cap, and the emerge/recede hysteresis never fires again after year
~10."* Measured **at HEAD, post-variance-fix** (dev worldgen seed 42, 900 turns, settlement 0,
post-boom window = emergence + 25 on; re-measured by the review-fix agent itself):

- **minimum post-boom surplus = 1.4561** (settlement 0), not > 2.0; the min over ALL settlements
  is **0.5357** — on that reading the recede threshold is *crossed*, elsewhere in the world;
- **42 post-boom turns with surplus < 2.0** on settlement 0;
- share: boom peak **0.2137**, post-boom minimum **0.1653**, end **0.1997** — it does *not* pin at
  the 0.20 cap, and it does not stay fallen either;
- the recede predicate `food_surplus_ratio < 1.1` is **never crossed on settlement 0** — missed by
  ~32% at the surplus minimum.

**CORRECTION OF THIS FILE'S OWN FIRST VERSION (§7.12, recorded).** The figures this section
previously carried (min 1.114, 90 sub-2.0 turns, share 0.196 → 0.139, "missed by 1.2%") were
T3.4b-pin measurements published in the present tense in a post-fix commit — the packet corrected
one unmeasured claim by writing another. The dramatic conclusion built on them — *"the recession
valve now very nearly fires, and the family is one bad draw from un-quarantining itself"* — is
**withdrawn**: at HEAD the settlement-0 margin is ~32%, not 1.2%. What survives: the quarantine's
stated reason ("stays above 2.0 all campaign") remains false, the conclusion (share never drains
below 0.05) remains true, and the mechanism claim remains an un-re-measured carry-over.

### 3. Family 6's supporting justification cited evidence deleted two commits later

`429f33c` justified holding the chronicle family by pointing elsewhere: *"famine IS now reported
elsewhere: the grievance test's fed prelude accrues 4.80 where it was exactly 0."* Commit `0a4c374`
then excluded weather from that very rig and restored `Assert.Equal(0.0, ...)`. **At HEAD the 4.80
does not exist.** The primary reason for the quarantine is true and independently measured
(`anyFamine = False` on a live 900-turn world); the supporting claim is void and is withdrawn here.

It was also an overstatement when written: 4.80 was *grievance accrual*, not a famine report.

### 4. Migration teeth — a compound claim, half wrong

`MigrationTests` states the tooth was restored by *"the T3.4b re-derivation … **and harvest variance
opened real attractiveness gaps**"*. Measured against the `hot > canonicalFull × 1.5` bound:

| config | ratio (bound 1.5) |
| --- | --- |
| shipped | 1.569 |
| **σ = 0** | **2.217** |
| σ = 0, old landWeight | guard fires — hot below the absolute floor |

**Weather is not needed for the tooth, and it SHRINKS the margin toward the failure bound.**
`landWeight` alone restores it. The comment names `landWeight` first, so this is a compound claim
half-wrong rather than wholly wrong — but the live margin is 4.6% and the packet credits it to the
one factor that is eroding it.

## Why all four corrections share one cause

Weather and the migration weight re-derivation **landed in the same commit** (`11a1800`). Every
attribution above credits the headline change without isolating it. The general lesson is ADR-015
§7.10's — a measurement and its interpretation verify separately — with a specific corollary worth
stating: **when two mechanisms ship together, no effect may be attributed to either without a
single-variable control.** σ → 0 is that control here, and it was available the whole time.

## Anchors

`ClassSystemTests` records *"harvest variance moved it 70 → 81"* and re-anchors the emergence window
to `[30, 95]`. Measured at the T3.4b pin: **76**, not 81 — the comment was written in `429f33c` while
`sigmaLogYield` was still 0.18, and the next commit derived 0.2936 without re-measuring it. The "70"
was never a measurement either; it was the *upper edge* of the old `[30, 70]` assert, and the true
pre-weather value on this tree is 54. **The window `[30, 95]` still holds; its stated derivation does
not reproduce.** T3.4c does not move the window — that would be tuning a bound to match a corrected
story, and the bound is not what is wrong.

At HEAD, post-variance-fix, the emergence turn measures **84** (review-fix re-measurement) — moved
again from the 76 measured at the T3.4b pin, still inside `[30, 95]`. The window continues to hold;
the anchor's narrative value keeps moving with the substrate, which is exactly why the bound and not
the anecdote is what is asserted.
