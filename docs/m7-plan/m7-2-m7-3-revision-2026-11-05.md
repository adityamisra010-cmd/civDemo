#### C1 Time model: four clocks on one integer timeline (revised)
- **Invariant.** Four clocks: campaign turn (era dt), Operational War Pulse (era-varying, fixed duration, local to a war), tactical battle pulse (fixed length), internal simulation step. War Pulses may cross turn boundaries; crossing is realized by sub-stepping (C21). Decision windows open at non-integer positions within a turn (C2).
- **Source.** DD: DIR5 §2.4, §2.5; D-076 N1–N4; R28–R31; ledger §19 (`ledger:943`, "War Pulses can cross strategic turn boundaries").
- **Delegated.** `SimInstant(long Ms)`; turn tiling from `Sim.Data/content/era-pacing.json` (dt 3600/1800/1080/720/360/180 days); a War Pulse's length is keyed on the era band containing its start instant and kept for the whole pulse (READING for Q-T2); the internal-step scheme (hybrid, D-076 N3). Values: OD-4 (ii) War Pulse durations, OD-4 (v) tactical pulse length.
- **Tests.** Tiling over all 1630 turns; `TurnOf`/`PositionInTurn` round-trip; boundary pins (an event at a pulse edge lands in the stated pulse); pulse-length keying at a band edge; crossing invariance (one-span run = turn-by-turn run = a calendar with different turn boundaries, equal state and trace hashes); dimensional declaration (§7). #### C20 Fractional-time carry: the exact rational rule (new)
- **Invariant (DD, DIR5 §2.5).** "Preserve fractional time proportionally when campaign-turn duration changes. Do not discard leftover time."
- **Rule T1 — time is never fractional.** Every instant is an integer number of ms. Turns tile the timeline: S_{n+1} = S_n + L_n, with no gap and no overlap. A turn's length L_n is fixed at its start (`ledger:615`, "`dt` is immutable through a complete player cycle"); a change of duration takes effect at the next turn start.
- **Rule T2 — elapsed time is carried by the instant.** A decision window at instant T splits turn n into [S_n, T) and [T, S_{n+1}). The pause consumes zero sim time; S_{n+1} does not move. A War Pulse [P, P + W) keeps its length W whatever boundaries or duration changes it straddles; its parts in each turn sum to W exactly. Nothing can be lost at a duration change, because no time quantity is ever rounded.
- **Rule T3 — the carried fraction (the "leftover").** Every integer quantity accrued from a rate over campaign time is integrated as
  Q(t0, t1) = ⌊(ρ·(t1 − t0) + c) / D⌋, with the new carry c′ = (ρ·(t1 − t0) + c) mod D,
  in `Int128` intermediates, where the rate is the exact rational ρ / D units per ms, ρ ≥ 0 and D > 0 are integers, and 0 ≤ c < D. The carry represents exactly c/D of one unit.
  - For a per-sim-year rate (Law 3) of r = a/b units per sim-year, D = b · Y with Y = 31,104,000,000 ms per sim-year, and ρ = a. D does not depend on the turn length, so **a change of turn duration leaves c unchanged** and the fraction c/D is preserved exactly. That is proportional preservation with zero loss.
  - Split invariance: ⌊(X + Y′)/D⌋ = ⌊X/D⌋ + ⌊((X mod D) + Y′)/D⌋, so integrating over any partition of [t0, t1) (pulse boundaries, decision windows, turn ends, band changes) gives the same total and the same final carry as one integration.
- **Rule T4 — quantities denominated per turn.** Where a quantity is denominated per campaign turn (only agency budgets under A-15, and the prototype's per-turn demo rates), the carry c over L_old is re-expressed over L_new by the exact rational identity c_new / L_new = c_old / L_old. To keep this exact in integers, the carry is held over **D_T = lcm of every turn length (ms) the era table can produce**; a per-turn quantity of R units per turn accrues R · Δt · (D_T / L_n) numerator units over Δt ms of turn n (an integer, because L_n divides D_T), and Q = ⌊(numerator + c)/D_T⌋ as in T3. Since c/D_T does not depend on L, the identity holds with no rounding. For the shipped table D_T = lcm{3600, 1800, 1080, 720, 360, 180} days = 10,800 days = 933,120,000,000 ms (below 2^63 ≈ 9.22·10^18). The era-table loader computes D_T from data and rejects a table whose D_T does not fit in `long`.
- **Rule T5 — campaign `double` systems.** Systems that stay `double` (D-004 retained for campaign systems) receive a sub-step's dtYears = (t1 − t0)/Y from exact integer endpoints, by one IEEE division (correctly rounded, so platform-equal). Their banked remainders (the `ConservedMath` pattern, `Sim.Core/Kernel/ConservedMath.cs:30`) are fractions of a unit, not of time, and are never rescaled at a duration change. Rounding inside a `double` integration is not time loss; it is covered by the re-pins (DIR5 §2.4) and by C11.
- **Superseded mechanism.** The prototype's ⌊c · L_new / L_old⌋ (`prototypes/m7-sprint1/reports/B-foundation.md:40`; `prototypes/m7-sprint1/Proto.M7/Demo/CampaignDemoWorld.cs:131`) discards up to one numerator unit at each band change and is replaced by T3/T4.
- **Delegated.** The choice of D per quantity (T3 or T4); the exact bound checks.
- **Tests.** Tiling over all turns; pause invariance (a decision window with no orders leaves S_{n+1} and the turn's elapsed ms unchanged); split invariance over 200 random cut sets; band-edge carry (a process accruing across every band edge in the shipped table matches an independent big-integer computation, with c unchanged in T3 form and c_new·L_old = c_old·L_new exactly in T4 form); zero-loss metamorphic (accrued units + c/D equals ρ·(t_end − t_start)/D + c_0/D as an exact rational over long horizons with many duration changes, interrupts and pulses); a mutant applying ⌊c·L_new/L_old⌋ must be killed by a semantic test; loader rejection of an overflowing D_T and of non-integral dt days.
- **Home / packets.** Campaign kernel (A-13) and every campaign accrual. M7.3, M7.14.

#### C21 Sub-stepping and per-pulse economic effects (new)

No system-private economic clock; sub-calculation inside one step stays private. Per-turn grants are keyed to the campaign turn, so sub-stepping never multiplies them. **Mortar counted object — DELEGATED technical proposal (for M7.6; not a Director decision).**
- **Counted object:** the crew-served mortar **weapon team** = one tube plus its crew. The cap counts weapon teams: at most 5 per formation. This follows the REV wording "5 weapon systems/crews" (REV:402), read as one crew per system.
- **Representation:** under V1, no tube stock is persisted. A mortar formation persists only its personnel P. At battle start it instantiates T = min(5, ⌈P / crew⌉) weapon-team entities, filling full crews first; the last team may be short-crewed, and its rate of fire scales with crew present as a coefficient (Law 2). `crew` is a per-identity TUNE value, not set here.
- **Validation:** ⌈P / crew⌉ ≤ 5 at recruitment, merge, replacement and load; a split must leave both parts within the cap.
- **Casualties:** losses reduce P; after battle the formation keeps only P (C3, C24).
- **Alternative counted object:** personnel (5 people). Not proposed, because it contradicts "weapon systems/crews".
- **Tests:** cap validation on every lifecycle path; instantiation determinism (same P → same teams); short-crew rate scaling; conservation of P across battle.
