## Q1 — THE LAND-CAPACITY / LABOUR-CAPACITY BIND RATIO

**Observable:** per settlement, the ratio `landCapacity / labourCapacity` in the production
equation on the canonical world, at a stated turn.

**COMPOSITE? YES.** "The bind ratio" hides three things and they are reported SEPARATELY:
1. the **land** term as the code computes it (arable × yield-side factors);
2. the **labour** term as the code computes it (farmers × output-side factors);
3. **which arm the `min()` actually selects**, per settlement per turn — the ratio can sit near 1
   while one arm always wins, and a ratio far from 1 tells nothing about frequency.

**PRE-COMMITTED READINGS:**
- **ratio ≫ 1 (labour binds everywhere, by orders of magnitude)** → the shipped world is nowhere
  near land-capped; B-2 and colonization have a correspondingly large distance to close, and the
  number quantifies it. **Expected**, given T3.4c needed ×1e6 to force the other arm.
- **ratio near 1 anywhere** → some settlement is close to land-binding today, which would be a
  **surprise** and changes what B-2's bounding has to reach.
- **ratio ≪ 1** → land already binds and T3.4c's rig was measuring an already-bound world; that
  would be a DEFECT-class finding against the T3.4c record.

## M3 INHERITANCE A — THE ARTISAN CLASS COLLAPSE, AND THE CHAIN IT GATES

**Observable:** the chain, measured end to end and reported link by link:
`artisan count → artisan_share → the bronze gate → bronze → tools → the farm yield multiplier`.

**COMPOSITE? YES, and severely** — it is a CHAIN, and a chain reported as one number hides which
link is broken. Each link is measured and reported separately, with its threshold where one
exists (the gate is stated as `artisan_share > 0.05` and that threshold is VERIFIED against code,
not carried from the prompt).

**PRE-COMMITTED:**
- **the chain is shut at the artisan link** → the collapse is upstream of everything else, and
  bronze/tools scarcity is a consequence, not an independent defect;
- **the chain is shut at a later link even where artisans survive** → there is a SECOND defect,
  and the artisan collapse is not the whole story;
- **the chain is open somewhere despite the collapse** → the gate is not what the prompt says it
  is, and the threshold claim is the thing that was wrong.

**MEASURE THE CHAIN; DO NOT FIX IT.**
