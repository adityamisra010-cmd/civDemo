# T3.4 — required-lens manifest

Written **before** the review runs, per ADR-015 §7.3 ("enumerate the lenses the packet REQUIRED
… not reconstructed afterwards from what came back"). At handback, each row is checked against
the lenses that actually RETURNED RESULTS — not against the launch call, which is exactly what
succeeded in T3.3 round one while the lens did not. **A missing lens is a BLOCKING condition.**

T3.4 is adversarial-mandatory and is the project's #1 flagged risk. Five lenses minimum.

| # | lens | what it attacks | required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `no-global-solve` | D-033's fixed mandate. Verify no hidden global equilibrium solve crept in — no cross-settlement price coupling, no iteration-to-convergence, no matrix solve, no all-goods fixed point. Per settlement, per good, one damped step per turn. | YES |
| 2 | `stability` | Reviewer **designs a destabilizing config** (λ large, band wide, market scale small, shock train) and tries to induce sustained oscillation or divergence inside the clamps. | YES |
| 3 | `oscillation-detector-vacuity` | The detector itself. Does it fire on a known-oscillating series? Would it pass a constant series, a monotone series, a series with one flip? Named separately because the packet's own acceptance depends on it, and a detector that cannot fire certifies anything. | YES |
| 4 | `dt-determinism` | Law 3 and law 5 on the price step: per-sim-year λ integrated with `dtYears`, equal-horizon totals across dt, replay equality, no `Dictionary`/`HashSet` iteration, ordering with `(score, id)` tie-break if any argmax appears. | YES |
| 5 | `conservation-and-explainability` | Law 1 is not directly at risk (prices are not conserved stocks), so this lens carries the **glass-box law**: does a price change actually decompose into its contributing terms, do the terms SUM to the observed change exactly, and is the decomposition load-bearing rather than a re-derivation of the same expression? | YES |
| 6 | `test-power` | Mutant survival across the whole packet. **Explicitly listed** because this is the lens that silently never launched in T3.3 and produced that packet's worst findings. | YES |

Six required, not five: the spec's floor is five and the T3.3 precedent argues for naming
test-power separately rather than trusting it to be folded into another lens.

## Mutation testing — bounds stated before the sweep (ADR-015 §7.1)

- Clean-suite baseline: measured immediately before the sweep and recorded here.
- Per-mutant bound: **5× the filtered-subset baseline**, hard timeout, killed at the bound.
- A mutant that exceeds the bound is recorded `M<n> hangs the suite; non-termination under this
  mutation`, with the cause in one line where cheap to determine.
- One worktree per verifying agent, never shared (ADR-015 §6).

## Handback checklist

- [ ] All six lenses returned results (by name, with raw/confirmed counts)
- [ ] Any lens that did not report is named as BLOCKING
- [ ] Mutant kill-record complete, with the baseline and the bound stated
- [ ] Every confirmed finding either applied or refuted with reasoning — **no finding acted on
      before its verdict returned** (ADR-015 §6)
