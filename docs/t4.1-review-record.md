## Q1 — THE BIND RATIO. **LABOUR BINDS 12/12, AT EVERY TURN. The distance is ~9×, not 3× and not 1e5×.**

Measured on the canonical world, seed 42, no orders — reproducing only the two products from
`ProductionSystem.cs:208-211` using the system's own helpers (`BandViews.Adults`,
`Sectors.Share`), so the inputs cannot drift.

| turn | LAND binds | LABOUR binds | ratio (land/labour) range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | 0/12 | **12/12** | 119.10 – 213.45 |
| 300 | 0/12 | **12/12** | 35.87 – 63.33 |
| 650 | 0/12 | **12/12** | **9.36 – 19.21** |

**Reading: the pre-committed "ratio ≫ 1, expected" branch — and now quantified.** The world
converges toward land-binding over a campaign (213× → 9.4×) but **never reaches it**. The answer
Q1 was filed to get: **at the end of a full campaign the shipped world sits roughly an order of
magnitude from land-capped**, closing steadily. T3.4c's ×1e6 was ~5 orders past the threshold; the
real threshold is ~10×.

**Q1.2 — A SECOND FINDING INSIDE Q1, not asked for and worth more than the headline: THE TOOL
YIELD BONUS IS EFFECTIVELY INERT.** `toolFactor = 1 + 0.3 × min(1, toolStock/farmLabor)`. Measured
at turn 650: tool stocks per settlement **0–49** against farmLabor of **2,000–4,500**. So
`equipRatio ≈ 0.001–0.01` and **toolFactor ≈ 1.001–1.015 against a designed maximum of 1.3** —
the mechanism delivers **under 5 % of its design range**, and in 3 of 12 settlements the stock is
0–2, i.e. exactly nothing. **CLASS: DEFECT-ADJACENT — "chosen, never derived" (c) on
`toolYieldBonusMax`, plus a quantitative gap no rule forbids.** Recorded and queued per S8 §4.1's
common-case disposition; **not corrected here.**

## THE DISCRIMINATOR THAT CHANGES BOTH M3 INHERITANCES

The M4 spec measured the artisan collapse on seeds 3/6/9 **replaying the shipped held-exit ORDER
LOG**. This audit re-ran the identical seeds **with NO ORDERS**:

| condition | emergence | AT EXACTLY 1 | zero at t650 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **with the held-exit order log** (M4 spec) | turns 4–137, counts bimodal {1,3} ∪ {26–60} | **19 of 35** | **6 of 12** |
| **NO ORDERS, same seeds** (this audit) | **turn 1, all 12** — counts 121–665 | **0 of 36** | **0 of 12** |

**BOTH M3 INHERITANCES ARE ORDER-CONDITIONAL.**

1. **The artisan collapse does not occur on the unordered world.** All 12 settlements hold
   thousands of artisans at turn 650 in all three seeds. **The collapse is driven by the labour
   orders, not by the emergence mechanism.**
2. **"Emergence at exactly 1" does not occur either** — without orders, artisans exist from
   **turn 1** at founding-endowment levels (121–665) and never emerge at 1. The bimodal split is
   **a property of order-driven re-emergence**, not of founding.

**CLASS: CORRECT BEHAVIOUR NOBODY EXPECTED**, and it re-aims T4.14 precisely: the subject is the
**interaction between labour orders and class emergence/mobility**, not the emergence rule alone.
Deciding rule: §7.10 — the measurement was correct in the M4 spec; the interpretation ("a class
going extinct across half the world") was missing its precondition. It goes extinct *when ruled
into doing so*.
