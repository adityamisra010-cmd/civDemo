## §1 THE THREE DECISIONS

### D1 — WHAT COUNTS AS UNPLACED DEPARTURE DEMAND? **RESOLVED. Current implementation retained.**

**The apparent conflict.** Part 4 said the mechanism "must not be derived from settlement
deficit". ADR-012 defines the Exit valve's desire as *"source-driven (`FamineFlightFactor ×
deficit_source`), uncapped by the gap mechanism, exactly as D-021 ratified"*. D-037 B1 places
colonization at that exact hinge: *"ADR-012 rules that with no viable destination people die at
home. **Extend it**."*

**Why it is not a conflict.** B1 names ADR-012's rule as the thing being extended, and ADR-012's
rule governs the **flight channel**, which is deficit-driven by ratified construction. The gap
channel cannot supply an alternative: a gap is `max(0, S_dst − S_src)` and **needs a destination
to exist**, so there is no destination-free gap desire to leave unplaced. **There is no
deficit-free source-driven migration desire anywhere in the ratified model.** Read literally
against the flight term itself, the prohibition would forbid implementing B1 at all.

The narrowest reading that satisfies ADR-012, D-037 B1, D-037 B3, CR-003 §5.2(a), the existing
`MigrationSystem` and laws 1/5 is therefore: **the prohibition bars deriving a founding trigger
from deficit *independently, inside colonization* — which is exactly what the defective
implementation did (`floor(count × deficit)`) — and does not bar consuming migration's own
already-ratified flight desire.** The two differ in the property that decides everything:

| | old trigger | current mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| computed where | inside colonization, from a deficit ratio | inside migration, as its own flight term |
| when a viable destination exists | **fires anyway** | **exactly zero** |
| dischargeable | no — a ratio is scale-free | yes — placing the party consumes a count |

**Retained as implemented.** The condition is **binary and is B1's own**: no reachable, viable
destination *at all*. §1.1 records why the graded alternative was rejected on evidence.

#### 1.1 The graded reading, examined and rejected — not merely declined
Viability is graded (`max(0, 1 − Repulsion × deficit_j)`) and damping is always `< 1`, so most
flight desire goes unrealised even when destinations are nominally viable. Reading "unplaced" as
*desire that was not realised* would fire far more often — and that is precisely why it fails:
`damping = exp(−cost/25)` is **never 1**, so under the graded reading a settlement in deficit
would have non-zero unplaced demand **every turn it is in deficit, forever**. That is the
scale-free, never-clearing shape of the original defect wearing different clothes. The binary
reading is the only one with a discharge condition.

### D2 — WHAT IS THE FOUNDING PARTY? **RESOLVED, and it exposed two real defects.**

Audited against each question the packet asked:

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| population stock the party comes from | the source's **live post-migration** `BucketRow.Count`, drawn key-for-key (D-026), moved only by `Ledger.Transfer` |
| fractional people | `ConservedMath.WholeUnits` floor, sub-person fraction banked in `UnplacedRemainder` (D-004) |
| remainder conservation | it is a **desire** accumulator, not a stock — see the defect below |
| can the source be drained below a valid state | it can be emptied, and **that is ratified**: ADR-012 says flight *"still empties a starving settlement into healthy neighbors"* and is *"a surge by design, bounded by the overdraw scaler alone"*. No new floor was invented |
| does the daughter start with a viable food stock | **it did not — this was a defect. Fixed.** |
| one-person settlement spam | not observed; and with the clearing cost binding, a party must be outfittable from a real granary |
| is the spacing floor sufficient against spatial spam | yes, and unchanged (§4) |

**DEFECT 1 — the remainder banked WHOLE PEOPLE.** `UnplacedRemainder` was assigned
`exact − take` **after** the availability clamp, so a bucket whose desire far exceeded its
population banked the difference and carried it forward. Measured during implementation: a bucket
with desire ~500 and 25 people **banked 475**, which would discharge later as one oversized
party. Fixed by taking the bank **before** the clamp, so it holds `exact − floor(exact) < 1` by
construction; desire the population cannot satisfy is dropped, since nobody moved and nobody is
owed a move. Pinned by `TheRemainderBanksOnlyASubPersonFraction_NeverWholePeople`.

**DEFECT 2 — ZERO-PROVISION FOUNDING, and it was disabling the cascade brake.** The old code
commented *"refugees leave with nothing"* and founded anyway when the source granary was empty.
That is not a cosmetic issue: **the brake IS the daughter's food.** A daughter holding provisions
satisfies ADR-012's absolute food gate, becomes a viable destination, and zeroes its founder's
demand. A daughter founded with zero provisions is non-viable, brakes nothing, and the founder
keeps founding. Fixed by making the **already-authorized clearing cost binding** — the same
per-capita share `TransferProvisions` computes, now *required to be satisfiable* rather than
silently clamped to nothing. **No new constant, no threshold, no timer**: one shared arithmetic,
called once as the precondition and once to move the grain, so "must be outfitted" and "this is
what it leaves with" cannot drift apart. Pinned by
`ZeroProvisionFoundingIsIMPOSSIBLE_AnEmptyGranaryOutfitsNobody` and
`EveryFoundedSettlementCarriesRealProvisions_NoEmptyDaughters`.

### D3 — FIRSTREIGN / ORDER SEMANTICS. **RESOLVED: it was DEFECT 2, not order semantics.**

**Order scope, established from source, not from naming:**
- `SectorAllocationRow(SettlementId Settlement, double Farming, …)` — `WorldState.cs:308`. The
  carrier is keyed by **settlement alone**.
- `OrderValidation.cs:26` — a `SectorAllocation` order packs `settlement × 8 + sector` into
  `TargetId` and is validated against the settlement table. The order **addresses a settlement**.
- A settlement with no row gets `Sectors.Default` (`WorldState.cs:375`) — the 55 %-farming
  subsistence mix — read that way by `ProductionSystem`, `HousingSystem` and `AppropriationSystem`.
- `BucketRow`'s key is `(Settlement, Culture, Religion, Class, CohortIdx)`. **No policy, order or
  polity dimension exists on people anywhere in the tree.**

**So the order is explicitly settlement-local, and no carrier exists by which it could follow
emigrants.** D-037 B1 independently confirms it should not: *"A settlement founded by departing
population NEED NOT belong to the polity they left — refugee foundings may be stateless."*

**But the question is now moot, and the honest answer is neither A nor B.** At `4d11c02` this
world founded 16 settlements over turns 6–19 and its shape asserts went red. That was **not** the
Exit valve operating correctly — it was DEFECT 2. The FirstReign source's granary is empty from
turn 5, so every one of those daughters was founded with **no provisions at all**. With the
clearing cost binding, **no founding is possible in that world**, the reign dies exactly as it
always did, and the test passes with **every shape assert intact** and at main's own trajectory.

**The shape asserts were never weakened, and they earned their keep: they caught a real bug.**

## §2 IMPLEMENTATION

```
food deficit at the source
  -> MigrationSystem forms flight desire   (FamineFlightFactor x deficit, per bucket)
  -> is ANY destination reachable AND viable?          (ADR-012's gates, unchanged)
       YES -> migration transfers.  UnplacedDeparture = 0.  Colonization does nothing.
       NO  -> UnplacedDeparture = the flight desire, per bucket, keys intact
  -> ColonizationSystem floors it to whole people (sub-person fraction banked, D-004)
  -> CAN THE EXPEDITION BE OUTFITTED?  per-capita share of the home granary > 0?
       NO  -> no founding.  The people stay.
       YES -> frontier site (ADR-018 spacing floor + explicit SiteCell distinctness)
  -> Ledger.Transfer of the party, key for key, plus its provisions
  -> the daughter holds food, so ADR-012's own gate makes it a VIABLE DESTINATION
  -> next turn the founder's demand is ZERO.  Founding stops.
```

**No cooldown, age threshold, minimum-population constant, founding probability, RNG stream, TUNE
constant or timer exists anywhere in the diff.** The brake is a mechanism: the old trigger made a
newborn **emit** a founding, this one makes a newborn **absorb** one.

`MigrationSystem` gains a **pure readout** and nothing else — it writes `UnplacedDeparture` after
every input it reads is final and before any transfer. No equation, flow, cap, gate, EMA,
attractiveness term, pair selection, remainder, overdraw rule or pipeline position changed. **That
is proven in §5, not asserted.**

One further guard, found by measurement during implementation: a **missing** `SettlementDistances`
row means "the network has not been computed yet", not "nowhere to go" — a genuinely unreachable
pair still has a row storing `+∞`. Treating missing data as isolation founds settlements out of an
empty table. `hasDistances` closes it; `n == 1` is exempt because being alone in the world **is**
the condition.

## §8 THE ONE REMAINING DIRECTOR DECISION

### R1 — D-037 B1 IS A COLLAPSE MECHANISM, AND CR-003 §5.2(a) NEEDS AN EXPANSION MECHANISM.

**Governing text.** D-037 B1: *"ADR-012 rules that with no viable destination people die at home.
Extend it."* CR-003 §5.2(a): T4.4 is *"how population converts empty land into settled, worked
land, and therefore how the frontier eventually closes and Malthusian pressure legitimately
emerges."*

**Code path.** `MigrationSystem.Step` — the flight term exists only as
`damping × viability × FamineFlight × deficit`; the readout fires only when no destination is
reachable and viable. `ColonizationSystem.Step` — consumes that and nothing else.

**Measurement.** Zero foundings and zero unplaced demand across 650 turns × 3 canonical seeds and
300 driven turns, on 13–19 deficit turns per seed.

**Why, structurally.** B1's condition is met only in near-total isolation or collapse. The
land-hunger driver that would actually close a frontier — *"in a frontier world the driver of
movement is LAND OPPORTUNITY"* (`sim.json` `_docLandWeight`, ratified under CR-003 §2.6) — lives
in the **gap channel**, and the gap channel **needs a destination to exist**, so it can never
produce unplaced demand. **B1 authorizes refugee founding; refugee founding is not frontier
closure.**

**Competing interpretations.**
- **(a) B1 is exactly what it says.** T4.4 as built is correct and complete, and CR-003 §5.2(a)
  needs a **second, land-driven mechanism** that B1 does not authorize — a new packet with its own
  ratified basis. *Implementation that follows:* keep this branch as-is; the Malthus corridors stay
  quarantined; write the expansion packet's spec.
- **(b) B1's hinge should be read more broadly.** Change only the readout's condition in
  `MigrationSystem`. *Implementation that follows:* the carrier and colonization are unchanged;
  §1.1 shows the obvious broadening (graded viability) reintroduces a never-clearing trigger, so
  this option needs its own discharge condition, which does not exist in the tree today.
- **(c) The carrier is not worth its cost.** If B1 is ruled collapse-only and frontier closure
  will come from elsewhere, **revert schema v22** rather than ship 16 bytes per bucket for a
  mechanism that cannot fire (ADR-021 says this explicitly).

**Smallest decision required:** whether D-037 B1 alone is expected to deliver CR-003 §5.2(a)'s
frontier closure, or whether a separate land-driven expansion mechanism is authorized.

**My recommendation: (a).** It is the only option that does not either reintroduce the defect this
packet exists to fix or read words into B1 that are not there. T4.4 as built makes B1 mechanically
real, conserves exactly, self-limits by mechanism, and moves no golden behaviourally — it is
finished as *B1*. What CR-003 is waiting for is a different mechanism, and saying so is more
useful than stretching this one until it fires.

**I am not making that call, and I did not choose it because it makes tests green.** Under every
option above, the tests are equally green.
