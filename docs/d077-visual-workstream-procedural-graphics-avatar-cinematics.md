## 1. Decisions (DECIDED, 2026-11-04)

| # | Decision (VIS, condensed faithfully) | Closes or touches |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Visual ambition: modern Total War-class 3D battlefield presentation. Procedural graphics are a major priority, "procedural generation with artistic quality, not … for its own sake". It covers terrain and biomes, structures with era styles and damage states, individual soldier and machine models with variation and animation, weather, day/night, effects and water. There must be no repetitive cloned assets. | Extends R21/R22 (D-075) |
| V2 | Graphics starts now, in parallel with the foundational simulation. "Do not … defer all graphics development to the end." | Supersedes the reading that M7 graphics follows the simulation |
| V3 | **Avatar:** a fully customizable human character with face, features, body, clothing, armour and accessories. **Identity:** the player personally, persisting across eras. It is not replaced on dynasty, ruler or political change. Era-appropriate visual evolution with the same identity. Used in victory and defeat cinematics, diplomatic encounters and important campaign events. It need not be a battlefield unit. | New |
| V4 | **Contextual victory cinematics** generated from the actual war outcome and post-war state. They must respect surrender, capture, execution and peace. "Do not show an execution if the defeated leader survived and surrendered." "Do not show a false victory if the war ended inconclusively." They use the persistent avatar, the opposing leader or representative, the victor's banner and war information. They are built from reusable authored animation components, not new animation per war. | New |
| V5 | **Cinematic camera, Total War-style:** pan, rotate, zoom, follow, track projectiles and events, frame charges, transitions, battle intros, victory and defeat sequences. "Camera movement, rendering quality and animation must never affect authoritative simulation outcomes." | **Closes O-27 → (b)** (camera cinema in M7) |
| V6 | **MonoGame explicitly selected.** "Do not propose switching to Godot." The renderer consumes state and events and determines no outcome, identity, movement, RNG or combat result. | **Closes O-13 → (a)**, a 3D path in the MonoGame host. Supersedes D-076 §1.2's carried boundary "Do not assume MonoGame or Godot" as to MonoGame |
| V7 | Proposed boundaries (Sim.Battle, Sim.Rendering, Sim.Assets, Sim.Animation, Sim.Cinematics, Sim.Avatar) are **to be investigated and recommended, not created**. | O-14 stays OPEN; a recommendation is due (§5) |
| V8 | A procedural framework: seeded generation, LOD, instancing and culling. Presentational generation never influences the simulation. Gameplay-relevant generated terrain and structures are explicit authoritative state, with deterministic generation and serialization. | Consistent with Laws 5–6 and D-076 S80 |
| V9 | **Performance target: the Director's HP Victus.** HW: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU (primary target) plus Intel Iris Xe (hybrid graphics), Intel CPU, 16 GB RAM (about 9.6 GB in use at desktop), NVMe SSD. Identify specs, baseline, bottlenecks and targets; "Do not sacrifice simulation determinism to improve rendering performance." | **Closes O-12 in part:** the reference machine is named. Numeric budgets stay OPEN until measured on it (Q-V4) |
| V10 | **Asset sourcing: procedural, purchased, commissioned and custom-rendered**, with provenance, licensing and compatibility metadata. Procedural variation enhances high-quality assets rather than replacing them. | **Closes O-25** (all four sources; provenance per asset, per D-069 R3) |
| V11 | Authorized now: inspect; prototype; an initial MonoGame 3D slice; procedural investigation; avatar and cinematic prototypes within bounded scope; builds, tests and benchmarks. **Not authorized:** wholesale architecture replacement; rewriting frozen simulation components; changing campaign or battle rules; release; commit, push, merge or tag. | Bounds D-076 §1.2's "Do not implement full 3D rendering yet": an initial slice is authorized; full 3D is not |
