## §1 WHAT SHIPPED

Comfort (need 6) stopped being satisfied by an annual basket draw of pottery and cloth and is now
satisfied by a **holding** of household goods.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Comfort's source | `basket` | `householdGoods` |
| satisfaction | basket fill ratio | `min(1, stock / requirement)`, requirement class-weighted over PREV buckets |
| pottery/cloth | eaten every year | **crafted into** a durable stock, drawn as materials |
| carrier | none — a flow | `HouseholdGoodsRow`, a conserved capital stock, schema **v22 → v23** |

**The one new constant** is `needs.householdGoods.serviceLifeYears = 5.0`, band 2–10, reference
class stated before the number. Everything else is arithmetic off ratified data: the holding
standard is DERIVED as `standard × wornFraction(1 year) = Σ perPersonYear`, and the former Comfort
basket lines were **moved verbatim** into `needs.householdGoods.perClass` because they are the
derivation input.

**Why this is not a second housing:** housing decays on unpaid maintenance — a SHORTFALL driver.
Here the driver is USE, and the whole asymmetry is one term: `inUse = min(stock, requirement)`
rather than `stock`. Idle surplus does not evaporate, surplus above the requirement buys nothing,
and **the equilibrium tracks POPULATION** — a growing settlement loses Comfort by dilution without
losing a single pot. That is m4-spec P4, and §7 measures it holding.

## §3 THE STATE TRANSITION, stated before implementation

```
requirement = Σ_classes  heads_c × standard_c              [units of household goods]
inUse       = min(stock, requirement)                      [units]
worn/yr     = inUse / serviceLife                          [units per SIM-YEAR]
crafted     = min(deficit toward requirement, materials available)
stock'      = stock − worn·dt + crafted·dt
Comfort     = min(1, stock / requirement)                  [dimensionless]
```

| | |
|---|---|
| stock owner | `HouseholdGoodsRow(Settlement, Units, CraftRemainder, WearRemainder)` |
| unit | units of household goods; conserved `long` (law 7) |
| source / sink | `ReasonIds.HouseholdGoodsCrafted` / `HouseholdGoodsWorn`; materials sunk under their own reason |
| producer / consumer | `HouseholdGoodsSystem` writes; `NeedsGrievanceSystem` reads |
| conservation identity | materials drawn == units made, EXACTLY, per settlement per turn |
| timestep | per-sim-year rates integrated with `ctx.DtYears`; **piecewise exact** (see below) |
| ordering | array-index iteration; largest-remainder allocation with ascending good-id tie-break |
| zero behaviour | no population and no row ⇒ skipped; population and no row ⇒ row materialised at zero |

**The dt integration is piecewise and that is the point.** Above the requirement the in-use set is
CONSTANT, so the rate is constant at `requirement/L` and the loss is linear in dt; at or below the
requirement it is proportional to the stock and e-folds. Law 3 is dt-**invariance**, not merely
"uses dtYears", and era pacing steps dt 10→5→3→2→1→0.5 — so the naive form would have produced
different Comfort purely from a world's era band.

## §4 CROSS-QUANTITY CONSERVATION — the invariant, reproduced against this tree

Crafting is ONE settlement-level pass with a **largest-remainder** allocation (ties by ascending
good id — a composite key, deterministic), and what is **sourced is what was actually DRAWN**, not
what was wanted. That is what makes `Σ materials drawn == units made` hold exactly rather than
approximately, and it is pinned by
`MaterialsDrawnEqualsUnitsMade_EXACTLY_NoMintFromRoundingSplit` and by
`OneUnitOfEachMaterial_CannotMintForever_PerpetualMotionPin` over 12 turns.

## §5 ADVERSARIAL PASS THIS RUN — the T4.4 interaction

**T4.4 is the first mechanism in the project that can append a settlement mid-run.** Every earlier
system, including this one, was written against a settlement table that was immutable after
founding. That is a genuinely new attack surface created by the rebase, and it had no coverage.

| new pin | what it attacks |
|---|---|
| `ASettlementThatAPPEARSMidRun_GetsItsHouseholdGoodsRow_AndConserves` | a settlement arriving the way colonization delivers one — settlement row plus buckets moved by `Ledger.Transfer`, nothing else. Had this system only ever UPDATED existing rows, that settlement would silently never hold, wear or craft household goods: **a permanent Comfort hole no existing test could have caught** |
| `ASettlementWithPopulationButNoMaterials_HoldsNothing_AndStillConserves` | the colonized shape at its harshest — real people, no craft materials, five turns. Must not mint, throw, or leave a half-built row |

Both green. **Verified in source**, not assumed: `HouseholdGoodsSystem` materialises a missing row
at zero when `requirement > 0`, so the newcomer is handled.

**Attacks covered by pins already in the suite:** conservation under crafting, under wear, and
under the material sink; multiple materials with fractional quantities; tiny stocks; stock at,
just below and above the requirement; zero requirement and zero stock; multiple classes and
multiple settlements; multiple dt values for the same elapsed time
(`WearIsDtINVARIANT_OneBigTurnMatchesManySmallOnes_InTheSurplusRegime`); repeated application;
deterministic ordering; serialization round-trip populated **and** empty; boundary class indices;
and cross-quantity accounting (§4, §2).

**No defect was found in this pass.** The mechanism's own defects were found by the two earlier
independent reviews and are fixed and pinned.

## §7 MEASUREMENT — the mechanism is alive, and P4 holds

Canonical founded world, seed 1, 650 turns:

| turn | 1 | 10 | 100 | 200 | 300 | 400 | 500 | 600 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rows | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| units held | 1,290 | 1,298 | 2,743 | 5,767 | 6,961 | 10,192 | 14,897 | **21,809** |
| population | 4,649 | 4,533 | 8,996 | 19,177 | 33,918 | 49,630 | 72,601 | 106,221 |
| **units per head** | 0.277 | 0.286 | 0.305 | 0.301 | 0.205 | **0.205** | **0.205** | **0.205** |
| crafted (cum.) | 1,290 | 10,992 | 162,067 | 514,728 | 1,019,908 | 1,557,758 | 2,344,547 | 3,495,691 |
| worn (cum.) | 0 | 9,694 | 159,324 | 508,961 | 1,012,947 | 1,547,566 | 2,329,650 | 3,473,882 |

- **It is a flow equilibrium, not a static stock**: 3.50M crafted against 3.47M worn.
- **The stock tracks population**: units rise 17× while per-head settles at **0.205 and stays
  there for 300 turns**. This is m4-spec P4 measured — the stock does **not** saturate at 1.0
  forever, because population is in the denominator and growth alone breaks the ratio.
- Per-head falls 0.305 → 0.205 between t200 and t300 and then holds: the material-bound regime
  engaging, which is the mechanism working, not drifting.
