## §1 WHAT SHIPPED

Comfort (need 6) stopped being satisfied by an annual basket draw of pottery and cloth and is now
satisfied by a **holding** of household goods.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Comfort's source | `basket` (default) | `householdGoods` |
| satisfaction | basket fill ratio | `min(1, stock / requirement)`, requirement class-weighted over PREV buckets |
| pottery/cloth | eaten every year | **crafted into** a durable stock, drawn as materials |
| carrier | none — a flow | `HouseholdGoodsRow`, a conserved capital stock, schema **v21 → v22** |

Two stages: **stage 1** the carrier (row, schema, clone, auditor, POPULATED-table test) with no
behaviour; **stage 2** the mechanism (`HouseholdGoodsSystem`, the needs re-wiring, the goldens).

**The one new constant** is `needs.householdGoods.serviceLifeYears = 5.0`, band 2–10, reference
class stated before the number (ethnoarchaeological vessel and textile use-life). Everything else is
arithmetic off ratified data: the holding standard is DERIVED as
`standard × wornFraction(1 year) = Σ perPersonYear`, and the former Comfort basket lines were
**moved verbatim** into `needs.householdGoods.perClass` rather than deleted, because they are the
derivation input.

**Why this is not a second housing** (the packet forbids a copy): housing decays on unpaid
maintenance — a SHORTFALL driver. Here the driver is USE, and the whole asymmetry is one term,
`inUse = min(stock, requirement)` rather than `stock`. Idle surplus does not evaporate, surplus
above the requirement buys nothing, and the equilibrium tracks POPULATION — a growing settlement
loses Comfort by dilution without losing a single pot. That last sentence is m4-spec P4.

### 2.2 Stage 2 — THREE DEFECTS, ONE CRITICAL. All fixed in `fd1f059`.

**F1 — CONSERVATION MINT (law 1). CONFIRMED by execution.**
Units were sourced under one flow while each material was drawn under its OWN floor, and
`Σ floor(made × mixⱼ)` is strictly less than `made` whenever a share is fractional. Measured before
the fix: **1 unit minted per class per settlement per turn** with ample materials, and — the worst
case — a settlement holding exactly **1 pottery and 1 cloth crafted a unit every turn while its
material stocks never moved**. Perpetual motion.

`ConservationAuditor` is **structurally blind** to this: the source flow is individually legitimate
and the auditor reconciles each quantity against its own ledger, so a cross-quantity accounting hole
does not register. That is why the shipped conservation test passed. Recorded because it is a
general lesson about the auditor's reach, not a fact about this packet only.

**FIXED** by restructuring crafting into ONE settlement-level pass with a **largest-remainder**
allocation (ties by ascending good id — a composite key, deterministic) so the draws sum to the
units made exactly, and by sourcing what was actually DRAWN rather than what was wanted.

**F2 — dt-INTEGRATION ERROR. CONFIRMED by execution.**
`1 − exp(−dt/L)` is the closed form for decay proportional to the STOCK, but above the requirement
the in-use set is CONSTANT, so the rate is constant at `requirement/L`. Measured: one dt=10 turn lost
**2615** where ten dt=1 turns lost **920**. Law 3 is dt-INVARIANCE, not merely "uses dtYears", and
era pacing steps dt 10→5→3→2→1→0.5 — so the same world would have produced different Comfort purely
from its era band. **FIXED** with exact piecewise integration: linear while stock > requirement,
e-fold at or below it, reducing to the plain e-fold in the sub-standard regime.

**F2b — AND THE TEST HAD THE WRONG AIM (ADR-015 §7.2).**
`IdleSurplusDoesNotEvaporate` asserted `req × wornFraction(dt)` — it **pinned the integration error
as intended behaviour**. Teeth are not aim. Its expectation is corrected to the constant-rate
quantity; the property it exists for is unchanged and still asserted.

**F3 — DROPPED SUB-UNIT RESIDUE.** `CraftRemainder` was banked for one class only, so an
artisan-only settlement **stalled at 1 unit against a requirement of 1.4343 forever** with a million
units of material — a permanent grievance floor it could not buy its way out of. **FIXED** by
banking ONE settlement-level residue on the aggregate demand.

**F4 — unguarded class index** in `HouseholdGoodsSatisfaction`, now bounds-checked exactly as the
crafting side already was. **F5 — per-settlement allocations** in the step loop replaced by scratch
arrays allocated once at construction.

### 2.3 What review checked and found CLEAN
Determinism (no `System.Random`, `DateTime`, `float`, LINQ, `AsParallel`, `GetHashCode`, or
dictionary/hashset iteration; array-index iteration throughout). Mutation teeth: all four mechanism
mutants died against at least one SEMANTIC test each. The fixture updates to `NeedsGrievanceTests`,
`NeedsBasketTests` and `PipelineLoaderTests` are faithful, not weakened — provisioning is applied
symmetrically to both arms of every contrast test, the deliberately-unequipped settlement stays
unequipped, and no assertion was relaxed. Every claim about the tree verified: the 4 former basket
lines are gone from `baskets.entries` and present verbatim in `householdGoods.perClass`; the
loader's ambiguity guard genuinely refuses a Comfort need that declares both a source and a basket
line; `standard × wornFraction(1) = Σ perPersonYear` holds to 12 decimal places for both classes.
