## THE MECHANISM QUESTION THAT MUST BE ASKED FIRST (and it constrains everything)

**Migration cannot change WORLD population.** It is a `Ledger.Transfer` between settlements —
people conserved, law 1. So S3, which is a spread in *world* population across seeds, **cannot be
a direct migration effect.** Any shared cause must run through an INDIRECT pathway:

> misallocated migration → a settlement holds more people than its food supports → starvation
> deaths → lower world population.

That pathway is not hypothetical: it is exactly T2.13's starvation-magnetism shape (an emptied
settlement's per-capita attractiveness turning it into a magnet), and the post-crash ping-pong
entry in `queue.md` records the oscillation half of it at canonical rates.

**This is the discriminating prediction**, and it is what makes the test worth running rather
than a correlation hunt.

## PRE-COMMITTED READINGS (§7.13, §7.15 decomposition stated)

### R1 — does migration volume co-vary with population across seeds?

**Observable:** `corr(migrationGrossPerDecade, finalPopulation)` over the 20 nightly seeds.

**COMPOSITE? YES.** "Migration signal" is at least three things and they are reported
**separately**: gross VOLUME, within-run VOLATILITY, and net REDISTRIBUTION. A seed can have high
volume with no sloshing (steady one-way drift) or low volume with violent sloshing (small flows
reversing every turn), and those are different defects.

- **Strongly NEGATIVE** → consistent with the misallocation pathway: more migration costs
  population. The three signals plausibly share a cause.
- **≈ 0** → the pathway is not operating at this magnitude. S1/S2/S3 are then NOT one thing, and
  saying so is the finding. **This is a real possible outcome and is not a failure.**
- **Strongly POSITIVE** → migration tracks population for a benign reason (bigger world, more
  movers); the correlation is then a scale artifact and must be normalised before it means
  anything.

### R2 — is the oscillation real and seed-general, or was Thiathiariath one settlement?

**Observable:** per-settlement population SHARE per turn, from the T3.12a replay reporter, over
seeds spanning the measured density range. Oscillation index = count of sign flips in the first
difference of a settlement's share series, ignoring exact-zero steps (the `PriceSoakTests`
detector, reused rather than re-derived).

- **High-oscillation seeds are the LOW-density seeds** → S2 and S3 are the same phenomenon.
- **Oscillation uncorrelated with density** → S2 is real but independent; report it as its own
  finding rather than folding it in.

### R3 — is the starvation pathway actually carrying the load?

**Observable:** per-seed `starvationDeaths` total against that seed's migration volume and
final population. The pathway REQUIRES starvation deaths to be the transmission mechanism.

- **starvationDeaths correlates with migration AND anti-correlates with population** → the
  pathway is confirmed end to end, not merely consistent.
- **starvationDeaths ≈ 0 across seeds** → **the pathway is FALSIFIED** whatever R1 shows, because
  there is no mechanism by which migration could have cost population. R1 would then be a
  correlation with a different cause, and I must say so rather than keep the convenient reading.

**R3 is the one that can kill the hypothesis, and it is pre-committed for exactly that reason.**
