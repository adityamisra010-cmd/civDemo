## THE DISCRIMINATOR THE DIRECTOR NAMED — ANSWERED

**DESIRED FLOW SCALES WITH THE RATE.** 4,752.8 → 43,086.4 (**×9.07**) at 480; 6,511.9 → 82,844.8
(**×12.7**) at 95.2. **The rate lever is NOT dead upstream of migration.** Branch 3's premise is
false.

**REALISED GAP-DRIVEN FLOW ALSO SCALES, AND ABOVE THE TEST'S THRESHOLD.** 3,342.0 → 8,501.8
(**×2.54**) at 480; 2,944.2 → 9,125.5 (**×3.10**) at 95.2. **Both exceed the 1.5× the teeth test
requires — and 95.2 responds MORE than 480, not less.**

**CAP BINDING IS NOT SPACING-DEPENDENT.** 36.4 % vs 36.6 % at ×1; 74.4 % vs 70.3 % at ×10.
**Branch 1's condition — "cap binds far more at 95.2" — is FALSE.**

## WHY THIS STOPS RATHER THAN CONCLUDING

**No stated branch matches**, so per A6 the packet reports and halts rather than picking the
nearest:
- **Branch 1** (binds far more at 95.2 AND truncates the 10× response): **binding share is
  EQUAL across spacings** → condition false, even though truncation share does rise (29.7 % → 54.8 %).
- **Branch 2** (binding similar at both spacings → the cap is not it): **binding share is indeed
  similar** — but the branch's conclusion does not follow, because the cap demonstrably truncates
  (80–89 % of desire at ×10). Adopting it would state something the measurement does not support.
- **Branch 3** (desired flow does not scale): **false** — it scales ×9 to ×12.7.

## THE CONTRADICTION THIS EXPOSES, WHICH IS THE PACKET'S REAL RESULT

The teeth test measures **gross migration per decade** and reports **0.86 % against 0.80 %, a
×1.07 response**, at 95.2 km. **But the gap-driven flow it is supposed to be exercising responds
×3.10 at that same spacing** — well above its own 1.5× threshold.

> **The quantity the test measures and the quantity the cap governs are not moving together.
> Gap-driven migration DOES respond to the rate lever; gross migration does not.** Therefore
> **something uncapped and rate-insensitive dominates gross flow** in the phase the test samples.

**NOT MEASURED, and named as the next question rather than asserted:** the **source-level overdraw
scaler** (`MigrationSystem`: `scale = desiredTotal[srcRow] > prevCount ? prevCount /
desiredTotal[srcRow] : 1.0`) is a SECOND truncation, applied per source bucket after the per-pair
cap. At ×10 the desired outflow plausibly exceeds the source population, so that clamp — not the
gap cap — would bound the realised total. **Famine flight is the other uncapped term** and is
explicitly excluded from the gap cap by design.

**Three mechanisms are now in play and only one has been tested here.** The teeth comment names
two; the measurement points at a third it does not mention.

## WHAT IS AND IS NOT CLAIMED

- **Measured:** the four rows above, on the canonical world, with the probe reverted afterwards.

- **NOT claimed:** that the teeth test is wrong. It measures gross migration honestly; what is now
