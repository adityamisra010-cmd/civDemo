## The model (Item 2 — director ruling: MAINTENANCE, NOT ABSTRACT DECAY)

Housing is a STOCK of dwellings per settlement, conserved and Ledger-audited (its own
conserved-quantity id; built via Flow source reason HousingBuilt, degraded via Flow sink
reason HousingDecayed; the MATERIALS consumed are goods sunk via Ledger — conservation of
materials exact is an acceptance criterion). Per turn, from PREV state:
1. MAINTENANCE demand = dwellings × upkeep materials/year × dt. Materials actually available
   (and drawn, via Ledger sink) cover a fraction m ∈ [0, 1]; the UNMET fraction degrades:
   dwellings lost = dwellings × (1 − m) × (dt/τ_h) (integrated exactly; remainder banked).
   Fully maintained housing persists indefinitely (rebuilt in place — the equivalence with
   decay-plus-upkeep, expressed as an explicit resource draw, which is why it was ruled).
2. BUILD: construction-sector labor (and build materials) add dwellings toward demand
   (population/personsPerDwelling), never past a modest surplus cap (1.2× demand — houses are
   built for households, not stockpiled; stated as mechanism shape, not tuning).
3. SHELTER satisfaction rebinds to the STOCK: min(1, dwellings × personsPerDwelling / pop).
   The needs.json Shelter material-flow basket entries are RETIRED in the same change (their
   doc named this packet as their replacement). Stopping construction for one turn must NOT
   zero Shelter — asserted explicitly; it is the defect this packet closes.
Labor: housing maintenance+build draw from the CONSTRUCTION pool; PathBuild banks the
remainder, read via a published per-settlement housing-labor row with the standard §3.2
one-turn lag (no same-turn feedback, no system-to-system reference — law 6).

## Reference classes (rates FIXED here, before any measurement)

- **RC-H1 — dwelling lifetime without maintenance.** Neolithic/LBK timber-post and
  wattle-and-daub structures: archaeological rebuild-cycle estimates cluster at ~25–50 years;
  mudbrick (Çatalhöyük-type) 50–100 with CONTINUAL replastering. Band 25–60 years, central
  **τ_h = 40 years** (zero-maintenance e-fold). One Neolithic turn (10 y) unmaintained loses
  ≈ 22% of stock.
- **RC-H2 — maintenance share.** Rethatch every 2–5 years, annual replaster, member
  replacement: annual upkeep ≈ replacement-cost/lifetime ≈ 1/40 ≈ 2.5%/yr of build materials,
  band 2–5%/yr. Central: **upkeep = 2.5% of build cost per dwelling-year**.
- **RC-H3 — persons per dwelling.** Nuclear-to-extended households 5–8; central **6**.
- **Build materials per dwelling.** Timber-framed, earth-walled: **2.0 timber + 1.0 clay**
  (roof/frame vs walls/floor — the material MIX is the reference statement; the absolute
  scale is checked against shipped production: a 420-person settlement needs ~70 dwellings =
  140 timber + 70 clay against per-settlement timber output measured at ~24/yr — a ~10-year
  build-out from scratch at partial allocation, plausible for a founding generation).
  Maintenance draw at RC-H2: 0.05 timber + 0.025 clay per dwelling-year.
- Labor: build labor per dwelling and maintenance labor share come from the same
  construction-labor budget PathBuild uses (laborPerAdultPerYear); build cost **0.5
  adult-years per dwelling** (a household raises a house in a season with help — stated as
  CHOSEN within an order-of-magnitude frame, per S8 §4.1(c)).
