# T4.1e — DEPOSIT SAMPLING: THE CATCHMENT, NOT THE SITE CELL

**Cut from `main` `d4ce188`. ONE change: `WorldFounding` samples deposit channels over the
settlement's hinterland instead of at its site cell. `minSpacingKm` stays at its shipped 480;
`moistureDecayPx` stays 40.** Split from T4.1b on the director's ruling so that every golden here
moves for exactly one cause.

## WHAT THIS IS — A DEFECT REPAIR PREDATING 95.2 KM BY TWO MILESTONES

`GoodsTests` asserts deposit abundances are *"NOT all equal across settlements (the
comparative-advantage precondition)"*. **Measured on the canonical world at the SHIPPED 480 km
spacing: moisture at the site cell reads EXACTLY 1.0000 at ELEVEN of twelve settlements**, the
twelfth reading 0.9756. **The precondition has been passing since T3.2 on one settlement sitting
2.4 % below a ceiling**, and livestock, timber, fiber and hides have been **effectively constant
across the founded world for two milestones.**

**This is ADR-015 §7.5 verbatim — never assert on a quantity resting against its own limit.** And
the saturation is not accidental: settlements are sited for water access, and the moisture channel
is *"1 at the shore, fading with the same moisture curve"*. **Siting selects for exactly the
locations where this channel carries no information.**

**The spacing change EXPOSED this defect; it did not cause it.** At 95.2 km the twelfth settlement
was sited somewhere wetter and the assertion collapsed.

## THE STATISTIC, AND WHY IT IS THE ONE CHOSEN

> **AREA-WEIGHTED MEAN of the channel over the 50 km hinterland footprint, land cells only.**

**Derived from the DIMENSION, and `GoodsTests` WAS NEVER CONSULTED IN CHOOSING IT.** The statistic
was fixed and justified in the manifest before any measurement:

- **MEAN, not sum.** A deposit abundance is an **INTENSITY** — it multiplies extraction rates
  (T3.3) — so it averages. `CatchmentSummaryRow.EffectiveArableKm2` is an **EXTENT** in
  fertility-weighted km², *"summed over the catchment's lattice blocks"*, so it sums. **Summing an
  intensity over a catchment would make abundance grow with catchment SIZE — CR-002's shape
  exactly**, a per-unit-land property read as a total. Same footprint, different statistic, and the
  difference is forced by the dimension rather than chosen.
- **AREA-WEIGHTED:** equal-area pixels make the plain pixel mean the area-weighted mean. Stated
  because it stops being true for anyone who samples stride-4 lattice blocks instead.
- **THE 50 km HINTERLAND:** the same radius the catchment uses, so deposit and arable describe the
  same territory. **The inconsistency between the two models — one reading a disc, one reading a
  single cell — is arguably the underlying defect, and one of them was always going to be wrong.**
- **LAND CELLS ONLY:** ocean is not hinterland; including it would make a coastal deposit a
  function of how much sea a disc happens to contain — a siting artefact, not geography.

Determinism: a fixed row-major scan of a fixed disc, summed in index order. No RNG, no dictionary
iteration, no LINQ.

## FIRSTREIGN — RE-MINT, NOT REGRESSION, AND THE DISCRIMINATOR WAS RUN

`FirstReign` carries a hash pin AND shape asserts, and **the shape asserts sit AFTER the hash
assert, so the failing run never reached them** — a changed hash alone could not tell a re-mint
from a regression. **The hash was re-pinned and the test re-run so the shape asserts actually
executed: they PASS.** Extinction inside (5, 25], dead world frozen, **no food mountain**.

**The semantic claim was RE-VERIFIED against the new trajectory, not carried.** That is the
T3.8 re-mint/regression separator, applied as written rather than cited.

## §7.10 INSTANCE AGAINST THE DIRECTOR, RECORDED AT HIS INSTRUCTION

The T4.1b widening's branch set enumerated **heterogeneity outcomes only** — restored, still
uniform, partial — and **never accounted for the obvious downstream consequence that changing
deposits moves world hashes.** The branches themselves were correct; **the enumeration was
incomplete**, and A6 caught what the enumeration missed by halting on a result no branch described.
**The rule that saves a packet is not the branch that fires — it is the one that catches the case
nobody wrote.**
