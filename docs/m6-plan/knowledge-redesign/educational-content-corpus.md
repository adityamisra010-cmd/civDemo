Gate rule (PROPOSED, enforced by validator C10): an entry ships only when its status is `source-checked` and every
listed claim is `supported`, `corrected` or a sourced `disputed` point that the text presents as disputed. C11 refuses a
status that claims more than its evidence (e.g. `source-checked` without a page-read source, `search-corroborated` with
an unsupported claim or resting only on low-confidence sources).

**2026-10-23 (Director review items 3–4).** `search-corroborated` and `source-checked` stay distinct by rule, not by
convention: C11 now requires **each** supported or disputed claim of a `source-checked` entry to cite a source the
register records as `page-read` or `document-read` — one opened page cannot certify claims resting on search results —
and a `source-checked` entry must itemise its claims. A source URL added to the register changes nothing by itself:
neither the validator nor `sourcing_queue.py` derives a status from citations (adversarial fixtures:
`tools/test_validate.py`, 25 fixtures, 15 mutants killed; `tools/test_sourcing_queue.py`, 8 tests, 5 mutants killed).
Per node, what each text rests on and every unresolved claim: **`corpus/unresolved-claims.md`** (generated by
`tools/unresolved_claims.py`): 169 unresolved claims (129 unsupported, 40 disputed) in 106 entries; 198 entries whose
whole text is unresolved; 205 entries above `unverified` whose claims are not itemised (status rests on per-source
claims; 60 of them do not record which statements are unchecked); 0 claims supported by a page that was read.

## 3. Editorial fact-check checklist (for the sourcing pass and KR-7)

1. Every date, place, person and quantity is supported by a source that was opened (not only a search summary)
   before shipping; ranges and "by about" where sources disagree.
2. Multiple-origin developments name more than one centre; contested origins and priorities say so.
3. Observation, explanation, commercialization and adoption are not conflated.
4. No anecdote, quotation or "first" without a source; quotations ≤ 10 words.
5. The text matches the node AS PROPOSED (split parts describe only their part) and its `mechanicLink` matches the
   capability block and its realization class (`capability-dependency-matrix.md` §3a).
6. No inevitability, no game language, neutral tone for weapons, regimes, slavery and epidemics.
7. Emerged-date conflicts recorded in `uncertainty` are resolved in the node record.
8. An independent check of a stated sample (recommended ≥ 20 %, all A9 nuclear and all regime forms) before KR-7 ships.

## 4. Finishing the sourcing (IMPLEMENTATION DEFERRED)

Work the queue in `corpus/sourcing-queue.md` batch by batch with the same rules, then re-run
`merge_sources.py → validate.py → sourcing_queue.py → unresolved_claims.py → render.py`, then
`test_validate.py` and `test_sourcing_queue.py`. Reaching `source-checked` requires the environment's
network settings to allow the source hosts (docket item B-7); until then the ceiling is `search-corroborated`, which does
not pass the gate.
