## 2. Design rules

- R-C1 Distinct tree: no civic copies a technology node; a civic may require technology where the knowledge is causally needed (exposure edge civic→technology); no technology prerequisite names a civic (V04 fence untouched).
- R-C2 No new civic enters the prerequisite closure of a ratified gate — the research stage, every Age CORE fact, the tax edict, inst.university (checked: 0).
- R-C3 Macro civics are leaves: nothing requires a macro and no macro requires a macro (no regime ladder); 10 of 18 carry OR routes in their own prerequisites.
- R-C4 No civic is added to any Age fact; macros are never Age facts; no Age entry grants a civic (I-6, V-08).
- R-C5 I-7 tagging: progressionRole is gate · age-supporting · module · optional · regime-form; an 'optional' civic is in no gate closure and no macro closure, so no macro's prerequisites are reachable only through optional nodes (checked).
- R-C6 Non-orphan under the current loader: every new civic carries a capability string (ResearchContent.cs:591-600); institution unlocks are listed only where an existing entity's requirement names the civic (reverse index, :1246-1255) — so none until a re-point is ruled.
- R-C7 Every promise names an owner system and a milestone; promises needing a representation the loader lacks (regime:/policy: grants, new inst.* entities) are IMPLEMENTATION DEFERRED (Civics may unlock only Institution entities, ResearchContent.cs:1240-1242).
- R-C8 Effects change conditions, never outcomes (ADR-019 addendum; law 2): each deferred effect names the equation, predicate or verb it will enter; no bonuses, no policy cards (V-29); governments are module sets, not enums or bonus bundles (Spine :85; D-042 §5).
- R-C9 No civic depends on a treasury (V-22; CR-008): central_banking is knowledge-only; social insurance and public debt are rejected candidates.
- R-C10 Costs: six factors in quarter steps; the Age enters only as the modal prior inside difficulty and institutional (digest C-04, C-14); prereq_complexity by top-level conjuncts with an OR group counted once (C-10); a costAnalog and a per-factor note; no cost chosen to hit pacing (C-23).
- R-C11 Ids are snake_case and unique against every node id and every entity short id (a node id equal to an institution short id is resolved as the node, ResearchContent.cs:1147-1153); display names are unique.
- R-C12 Every new emerged text carries an era marker and no must-hold prerequisite is dated later (heuristic of ResearchContentTests.cs:486-525) — checked; the 83 marker-free pin is unaffected.
- R-C13 Civics carry no branch (ResearchContent.cs:422-445); strands are a civic-block field, not subtrees (D-044 R3). The UI makes one Civics lane per distinct domain (ResearchTreeLayout.cs:125-131): all new civics keep domain 'governance' (existing vocabulary) — strand lanes are an OPEN DECISION (OD-10).

## 5. The research-stage civic atom

**Question.** D-061.8 (widened): should the civic legal_code_roman stay an atom of the research stage (researchStage.requires, research.json:254) now that civics may be paced by a separate currency?

**Measured baseline.** One-pool AI polity (measurements/baseline-summary.json ai_polity): legal_code_roman completed at turns 635/649/644/639 and the stage at 702/716/710/705 (seeds 1/2/42/7) — the civic atom never bound the stage; geometry_axiomatic did. Civic slack 66-67 turns on every seed.

- **Option A — Keep the civic atom, with a measured pacing guarantee.** No content change. T6.3 measures the tolerances below against the one-pool attribution twin; the AI civics lane works gate civics first. Pros: no change to ratified-derived content (D-044 R4 'university / research institutional stage'; the stage _doc derivation, research.json:255; ADR-029 §8); keeps one explicit, measured point where the two economies interlock — the institution of learning; today's measured slack (66-67 turns) and the cost ratio (civic 3,730 vs technology 24,930 = 0.15) make a stall unlikely under any CP rate ≥ 0.3 of RP. Cons: technology after A5 waits on the civics economy if CP is slow or the civics target is spent elsewhere; a human player who neglects civics locks all five subtrees — legal, but the UI must say so. Risk: depends on D-061.3 (CP inputs) and on an AI civics lane that exists only from T6.16.
- **Option B — Move the civic requirement from the knowledge-level stage to the realized university.** researchStage.requires → 'medicine_hippocratic AND (geometry_axiomatic OR algebra) AND stamp_seal'; inst.university keeps 'library AND legal_code_roman', so founding a university (and its research-cost seam) still needs systematic law; the stage _doc and ADR-029 §8 receive dated notes. Pros: CP pace can never stall technology knowledge; causally clean: specialized knowledge needs no law code; a chartered university does. Cons: re-reads a ★ settled ruling: 'the specialized branches become available when the civilization reaches the university/research institutional stage' (d044:145, ★ d044:796; digest S1-06) — the stage would become the knowledge half only; the stage expression itself is ratified content (D-046 F§1; digest S1-08) — a Director ruling; ResearchContentTests.cs:233-252 pins 'stage needs legal_code_roman'; goldens move where the civic lagged (attributed); stage closure falls from 18 nodes / 28,660 to 16 / 24,930 in the one-pool world too. Risk: a ratified-content change made to protect a pacing that has not been measured yet.
- **Option C — An OR alternative of civic routes to institutional learning.** researchStage.requires '… AND (legal_code_roman OR civil_service_examinations)' — the chartered-corporation route or the state-examination route. Pros: historically plural routes to higher learning. Cons: still paced entirely by CP — does not answer the question; puts civil_service_examinations (and paper, 11,830) inside a ratified gate, breaking R-C2; needs a D-047-style causal review of the new edge. Risk: adds a progression-relevant civic for no pacing benefit.

**Recommendation.** A, because the stage's institutional character is a ★ settled ruling (S1-06) and its expression is ratified content (S1-08): keep the atom, with tolerances P1-P6 as T6.3 acceptance, an AI civics lane that works gate civics first, D-061.3 aiming at CP ≥ 0.5 × RP (10,000 CP of other civics then costs about 17 turns, ESTIMATE), and a UI rule that marks legal_code_roman (and law_code) as 'opens the five subtrees' in the civics panel. B is the pre-registered remedy if P1-P4 fail after those remedies — it needs the Director to read S1-06 as the knowledge half of the university stage. C is rejected. The same guarantee covers the A5 CORE fact a5_systematic_law and the structural a5_wages gate (G-06).

## 7. Costs — micro/macro balance

Rule: macro ≈ the same Age's micro + 0.5…1.0 magnitude, i.e. ×1.4…×2 in cost

- Breadth: a form reorganizes several domains at once (participation, administration, legitimacy), so its downstream scope is civilization-wide where a micro's is one or two domains — the +0.5 breadth step.
- Institutional coordination: a form binds several modules into one arrangement — +0.25…0.75 over the institutional prior, noted per form.
- Difficulty stays at the Age prior: the hard part of a form (its modules) is already paid in the modules' own costs; the form's closure cost = modules + premium (meta.macroClosures).
- Bounded at +1.0: forms are optional alternatives; a larger premium would make 'optional' mean 'unreachable' for a civilization that develops several modules, and a premium below +0.5 would make a form a free relabelling of its modules.

## 12. Relocation candidates from Technology

**Verdict.** Keep every candidate in Technology; record recommendations only (crossLaneRequests). ## 14. Civilization VI — lessons only

- Two currencies, two trees, two targets (adopted shape, D-061) — but no flat yields and no policy cards (rejected, V-29).
- Give the civics currency something to buy from the first turns: four A1-A2 micro civics and two A2 forms.
- Avoid a strict government ladder: forms here are leaves over modules, reachable by OR routes, never prerequisites of each other.
- Cross-tree coupling there is through boosts only; here it is one explicit gate (the research stage) plus civic→technology prerequisites — kept explicit and measured.
- Government as mechanism: a form is the label of a module set whose effects enter named equations (arch-FGH), not a bundle of bonuses.
