### 374 · Crewed lunar flight (`lunar_flight`)

NASA's Apollo 8 orbited the Moon in December 1968, and in July 1969 Apollo 11's Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on its surface. Six Apollo missions landed between 1969 and 1972, returning about 382 kilograms of lunar samples.

### 397 · High-speed rail (`high_speed_rail`)

### 415 · Launch efficiency (`frontier_launch`)

Reaching low orbit means accelerating a payload to about 7.8 kilometres per second, roughly 30 megajoules per kilogram plus the climb. That energy is cheap; launch prices were historically dominated by hardware discarded after each flight, propellant handling and small production runs. Reuse, larger vehicles and higher flight rates have cut prices per kilogram; further gains are uncertain.
