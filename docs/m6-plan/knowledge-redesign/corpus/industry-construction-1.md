### 12 · Cordage (`cordage`)

Cordage is made by twisting plant or animal fibres into strands, then plying strands in the opposite direction. The oldest direct find is a tiny three-ply cord, probably of inner-bark fibre, on a Neanderthal stone tool at Abri du Maras, France, roughly 41,000 to 52,000 years old. Lashing, nets, snares and carrying depend on it.

### 50 · Mudbrick (`mudbrick`)
