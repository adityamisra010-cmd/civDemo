# Tranche 2 corpus — drafting notes (claims the drafters flagged as least certain)

The 442 entries in `tranche2-1.json` … `tranche2-6.json` (2026-10-24, D-063) were drafted from general knowledge with cautious wording; every entry is `factCheck.status = unverified` with an empty `sources` list, and its `factCheck.notes` names the claims to check first. Showing a text in the game does not mark it checked. Below: the claims each drafting lane itself flagged as most uncertain — the first sourcing targets. The three civics repeatables (civic_participation, institutional_reform, social_policy_research) keep the texts written with ADR-049.
