### 23 · Grinding stone (`grinding_stone`)

### 30 · Wheat and barley domestication (`cereal_domesticated`)

Domestication changed the plants themselves. In the Fertile Crescent, einkorn, emmer and barley gradually acquired ears that hold their grain until harvest instead of shattering, so they came to depend on human sowing.
