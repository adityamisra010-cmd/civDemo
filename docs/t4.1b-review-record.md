contradicts the Charter's ancient figure and would found ~457 settlements in 4000 BCE.

**Three shapes the director may choose between, none chosen here:**

## AND THE FINDING IS LARGER THAN THE PACKET — §7.5, EXACTLY

Look at the shipped row. **At 480 km, ELEVEN OF TWELVE settlements already read exactly 1.0000.**
The comparative-advantage precondition was passing on **one settlement being 2.4 % drier than the
ceiling.**

> **`GoodsTests`' comparative-advantage property has been passing on a single outlier since T3.2.
> Livestock, timber, fiber and hides — every moisture-channel good — have been effectively
> CONSTANT across the founded world all along. The spacing change did not break comparative
> advantage on this channel; it removed the last settlement that was keeping the assertion true.**

**This is ADR-015 §7.5 verbatim: never assert on a quantity resting against its own limit.** The
assertion rests on a channel pinned at 1.0, and it survived only because one sample fell 0.0244
short of the ceiling.

**And the mechanism is not accidental.** Settlements are sited for water access
(`waterAccessCutoffPx`), and the moisture channel is *"water proximity: 1 at the shore, fading with
the same moisture curve"*. **Siting selects for exactly the places where this channel saturates.**
The deposit model samples a field at the one class of location where that field carries no
information.

## WHAT IS AND IS NOT CLAIMED

- **Measured:** the twelve site values in all four combinations; the elevation contrast; the
  reverted state of the tree.
- **NOT claimed:** that the deposit model is wrong in design. Sampling terrain at the site is a
  reasonable model; **sampling a SATURATED channel is what fails**, and only the moisture channel
  saturates.
- **NOT claimed:** that 95.2 km is at fault. **The defect predates it by two milestones.**
- **NOT iterated:** no second value was tried. The pre-commitment forbade it and the diagnosis
  makes clear no value would have worked.

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE COUPLING RULING

The director ruled the deposit scale FOLLOWS the spacing, and that **two constants move together**.
**The measurement says there is no second constant to move on this channel** — the coupling is not
spacing-to-correlation-length, it is **siting-to-channel-saturation**, and it is a defect in the
deposit model's choice of field rather than in either constant's value.

**Three shapes, none chosen here:**
1. **Give the moisture-channel goods an explicit `depositSpread`**, as the ores already have —
   `goods.json`'s own `_doc` says spread is what *"makes ore geography uneven, the precondition for
   comparative advantage."* The ores were given it; the rest were left relying on a field that
   turns out to be flat. **This is the smallest change and it makes the implicit source explicit.**
2. **Sample the CATCHMENT rather than the site cell** — the catchment spans terrain the site does
   not, and is not selected for water access.
3. **Sample an unsaturated field** — fertility or a moisture gradient rather than moisture level.

**All three are design changes to the deposit model and none is mine to take.** Owner: the director,
and the natural packet is T4.3/T4.4's neighbourhood since comparative advantage is what the claim
and colonization models will read.
