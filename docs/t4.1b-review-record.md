## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE RULING — STATED, NOT DECIDED

The direction (spacing DOWN) **survives**, because the 4 % inconsistency is real and the late-game
Charter numbers are unreachable at 480. **But its justification changes**, exactly as M1's
pre-commitment anticipated: 480 is not a wrong constant, it is a constant **correct for the ancient
endpoint only**. Lowering it to ~95 km yields a late-game-density world **from turn zero**, which
contradicts the Charter's ancient figure and would found ~457 settlements in 4000 BCE.

**Three shapes the director may choose between, none chosen here:**
1. **Lower to a late-game value (~95–144 km)** and accept a dense ancient world;
2. **Lower modestly now** (a first step consistent with the playability coupling below) and take
   the rest when spacing derives from state;
3. **Make spacing DERIVE from computed state** (population pressure, land quality) per law 4 — the
   deferred item, which this measurement now shows is not merely nicer but **the only shape that
   satisfies both Charter endpoints.**
