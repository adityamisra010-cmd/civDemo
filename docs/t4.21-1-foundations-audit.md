## 2. Definitions (all from spec §2 and §3.3; nothing new)

- **ρ_t** (per settlement, per turn) = grain `LastProducedUnits` ÷ `ConsumptionDeficitRow.DemandUnits`
  of the same turn — the telemetry fields `food.harvest` (`SettlementRecord.cs:59`, "next grain
  LastProducedUnits") and `food.demandUnits` (`:63`). Both carry `dt`; the ratio is dimensionless.
  Turns 2–161 (turn 1's harvest is the endowment turn, 0). **ρ_t includes the realised harvest-weather
  multiplier of that decade** (F1 unfixed — CR-015 §6.4), so the per-turn value is the effective
  surplus ratio of that decade, and the time-mean over 160 decades of a mean-one multiplier is the
  proxy for the "weather-mean ρ" spec §3.3 speaks of.
- Because grain is 0.9 of the peasant basket (`needs.json:72-90`) and the non-staples are produced
  too, ρ_t understates the FEEDABLE surplus by up to the non-staple basket share. The exact feedable
  form is F5's fixed point (below); the two agree within 1 % everywhere except Libur under the
  director's orders (§4).
- **F4:** effective buffer at dt = 10, `B_eff = G + dt·(ρ − 1)` sim-years (`G = 1.5`); the event size
  a settlement absorbs, `sD* = (dt·(ρ − 1) + G)/ρ` production-years (spec §2 F4 / §3.3, weather = 1,
  opening store full). Since ρ_t already carries the decade's weather, `sD*(ρ_t)` is the exact
  per-decade threshold; `sD* ≤ 5.0` ⇔ `ρ_t ≤ 1.70` (the band's maximal event `s = 1` produces a
  deficit) and `sD* ≤ 3.75` ⇔ `ρ_t ≤ 1.36` (every event in the band does).
- **F5:** at the last turn, `X` solves `X = S + Σ_g min(NS_g, b_g·X)` with `S` = grain produced,
  `NS_g` = non-staple produced, `b_g = demand_g / D` (the `FoodHeadroom.Limit` form, spec §3.6a);
  `ρ_eff = X/D` against the sum form `ρ_sum = Σ produced / D` (mutant M-DEM-SUMFOOD).
- The band: `D = 5`, `s ∈ [0.75, 1.0]`, so `s·D ∈ [3.75, 5.0]` production-years; derived against
  `ρ_ship = 1.3` (declared, CR-015 §6.3 G8), whose threshold is `sD* = 3.46`.

## 3. What the measurement says (the facts the director must judge the band against)

1. **At its time-mean surplus, NO canonical settlement is exposed to the band without orders.**
   Across the four no-orders worlds (48 settlement series), the time-mean ρ is **1.78–2.02**
   (seed 42: 1.841–1.957), so `sD*(mean)` is **5.22–5.79 > 5.0**: the band's maximal event (5
   production-years) is absorbed by the decade-pooled buffer of every settlement in a mean-weather
   decade. Spec R14 predicted this ("a rich granary survives"); the number is now on the tree.
2. **With the director's 15 orders, exactly one settlement is exposed at the mean: Libur (id 10)**,
   ρ mean 1.431, `sD* = 4.06` — exposed to `s ≥ 0.81` (`s·5 > 4.06`), NOT across the whole band
   (`4.06 > 3.75`). No other settlement moves: all 15 orders are `SectorAllocation` orders at settlement 10,
   three batches of five at turns 21, 33 and 80 (the turn-80 batch sets farming 34 / herding 49 /
   extraction 6 / crafting 6 / construction 5), and the other eleven settlements change by ≤ 0.001
   in mean ρ, through migration.
3. **Exposure is decade-by-decade, through the weather.** The per-decade ρ_t has p5 ≈ 1.00–1.24 and
   min 0.69–1.04 (Libur under the orders: p5 0.77, min 0.51). A settlement is exposed to `s = 1` in every decade with ρ_t ≤ 1.70 — **31–51 % of
   decades per settlement (world 36–44 %)** — and to the whole band in decades with ρ_t ≤ 1.36 —
   **9–21 % (world 14–18 %)**. So at dt = 10 the famine-class property of the band is realised by COINCIDENCE of a
   strike with a below-mean weather decade, exactly as spec §3.3 states ("mandate 1(A) was
   reachable only when a disaster coincided with a bad-weather decade"), now quantified: a strike
   at λ = 0.01 lands in such a decade about 36–44 % of the time for `s = 1`, 14–18 % for `s = 0.75`.
4. **Where `ρ_ship = 1.3` sits.** It is below every settlement's time-mean (1.78–2.02), between the
   p5 and the median of the per-decade distribution (seed 42 p5 1.08–1.21, median 1.75–1.88), and
   it is Libur-under-orders' median (1.367). The declaration therefore describes Libur in the
   director's own playtest (as CR-015 says) and a below-median decade elsewhere — not the mean
   decade of any canonical settlement.
5. **F5 in the shipped Default mix is small; under the orders it is not.** Without orders,
   `Σ/X = 1.000–1.009` at turn 161 (the sum overstates feedable food by < 1 %). Under the orders
   Libur has `Σ/X = 1.519` (ρ_sum 2.148 vs ρ_eff 1.414): the sum form would have set Libur's
   headroom 52 % above the food it can eat. That is the spec's Libur example (`7458/0.9`) at the
   T4.21-1 tree.
6. **Consequence stated plainly for the band derivation.** With the F4 pooling in force at dt = 10
   and `hazardPerYear` restored, a strike on a mean-weather decade at a mean-surplus settlement
   produces `d = 0` (NORMAL — "decided by the food balance") at every canonical settlement in the
   four seeds without orders; the deficits the band produces come from strikes in below-mean decades
   (36–44 % of them at `s = 1`) and from settlements whose orders lowered their surplus (Libur).
   Making the maximal event beat the MEAN-decade buffer at the measured ρ (≈ 1.9) would need
   `s·D > 5.5` — outside `D = 5, s ≤ 1` — so it is not reachable by re-declaring `ρ_ship` inside the
   ratified band; the per-year food-balance sub-step queued under G8 is the change that would
   remove the pooling. This document does not choose; it reports.

## 5. Caveats (what these numbers are not)

- ρ_t is grain ÷ all-food demand; the exact feedable ratio is F5's `ρ_eff` (reported at turn 161
  only). Outside Libur-under-orders they differ by < 1 %; the exposure statements in §3 would move
  by less than that.
- `sD*` assumes the opening store is full (`G`). With the decade-pooled balance the closing store is
  capped at `G` every turn a surplus exists (`ConsumptionSystem.cs:195-230`), so this is the common
  case; a settlement entering a decade with a depleted store is more exposed than the table says.
- The "weather-mean" ρ is approximated by the 160-decade time-mean of a series whose multiplier is
  mean-one (the per-decade multiplier is not in the telemetry; `diag-telemetry.patch` in
  `docs/t4.21-evidence/` is the instrumentation that would expose it). The per-turn statistics are
  reported precisely so that nothing depends on the approximation.
- These runs have `hazardPerYear = 0` (T4.21-1 ships the disaster at zero hazard). The deficit
  columns are therefore the weather-and-orders food balance alone — they show where the world
  already runs a deficit before any disaster is possible (seed 2: 27 settlement-turns, 208 starvation
  deaths; seed 42 no orders: 7 and 19).
- Nothing here is a corridor or a target. The band stays as derived (spec §3.3, CR-015 §6.3 G8);
  this record exists so that the director judges that derivation against measured surplus ratios
  instead of against one declared value.
