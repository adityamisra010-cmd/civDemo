## 1. Decisions (DECIDED, 2026-11-05)

| # | Decision (QM, condensed faithfully) | Closes or touches |
|---|---|---|
| QM1 | The G1 technical foundation is useful, but its visual output is **not acceptable as the intended final visual direction**. "The current soldiers, faces, terrain and environments look like a basic low-poly prototype." | Refines D-077 V1 (S97) |
| QM2 | **Quality floor and ambition.** The target is "at least the visual quality of GTA San Andreas, with the longer-term battlefield ambition of Total War: Troy". It is a visual quality requirement, not a request to abandon MonoGame or the deterministic rendering architecture. **READING:** the named games are quality references only; no asset, character, art, likeness or level from them is used or recreated. | Refines D-077 V1 (S97) |
| QM3 | Placeholder assets are not the visual target. The next graphics sprint must not merely add more low-poly trees, rocks or blocky soldiers: "demonstrate a genuine increase in visual quality". | Supersedes the priority of the G1 next slice (S99) |
| QM4 | **Production-quality asset demonstrator:** one integrated battlefield with one highly detailed soldier, one highly detailed commander and one fully realized player avatar. It needs detailed faces, eyes, hair, skin, clothing, armour and equipment; high-quality weapons, shields and accessories; one detailed battlefield environment; a convincing forest; at least one settlement or fortification; proper terrain materials and environmental lighting; and realistic shadows and atmospheric depth. Soldiers and commanders have convincing human proportions. | Widens D-038 C1/C3 supersession to faces on the battlefield (S100) |
| QM5 | **Character pipeline:** investigate high-quality authored humanoid assets (glTF-compatible included). Implement skeletal animation, blending, locomotion, combat animation, weapon handling, reactions and hit animations, death and surrender animations, facial construction and expressions where feasible, and era-specific clothing and equipment. The avatar keeps a consistent identity across eras while appearance and attire evolve. | Extends D-077 V3 |
| QM6 | **Procedural generation multiplies quality.** High-quality authored base assets plus procedural variation. Soldiers vary in faces and features, skin and hair, armour and clothing, helmets, shields, weapons, banners, equipment, and body proportions within controlled bounds. Environments vary in vegetation, rocks and ground detail, terrain materials, structures, architectural styles and environmental effects. No repetitive cloned appearances. | Compatible with D-077 V1, V8 and V10 (READING, S98) |
| QM7 | **Rendering pipeline,** investigated and implemented as justified: PBR; high-quality material maps; normal, roughness and metallic maps; improved shadow mapping including cascades; anti-aliasing; ambient occlusion; atmospheric fog; better water; improved lighting and exposure; GPU instancing and LOD; occlusion culling; efficient animation and crowd rendering. "Do not add rendering techniques merely to increase technical complexity. Demonstrate visible improvements and benchmark their cost." | New |
| QM8 | **Repeatable visual benchmark scenes:** A soldier close-up, B formation (several hundred), C large battlefield (thousands, plus terrain, vegetation, structures and effects), D cinematic (two detailed characters interacting), E campaign environment (a detailed settlement and surrounding terrain). Screenshots and performance measurements at each stage. | New; scene E reading is Q-QM1 |
| QM9 | **Performance:** the HP Victus RTX 4050 remains the reference machine. "Do not use software-rendering benchmarks as evidence of Victus performance." Keep a separate record of GPU frame time, CPU frame time, memory and VRAM, draw calls, triangle counts, instance counts, animation cost and performance at different LOD settings. Keep rendering separate from the authoritative simulation. | Refines D-077 V9 and Q-V4 (S101) |
| QM10 | **Deliverable:** a visual quality review against the G1 screenshots, with before-and-after screenshots, asset provenance, rendering changes, animation improvements, performance measurements, remaining visual gaps and the recommended next graphics sprint. | New |
| QM11 | **Engine and bounds:** do not switch engines; MonoGame stays (reaffirms D-077 V6). No commit, push, merge, tag or release without authorization. Continue within the existing bounded prototype authorization; request approval where structural or protected boundaries require it. | Reaffirms D-077 V6, V11 |
