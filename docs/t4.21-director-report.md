## 1. WHAT WAS WRONG

The director played the M4 build and objected to what one bad harvest decade did to one settlement.
The packet's diagnosis, in the terms the code turned out to use, is five separate defects that
compounded. Each is stated here as the packet found it, not as the packet fixed it.

**1.1 The deficit was treated as a famine.** **CONFIRMED** (CR-015 §1 N1/N2, read on `45046eb`).
`ConsumptionDeficitRow.DeficitRatio` is a food-balance signal — how far this turn's feedable food
fell short of demand. Every downstream reader treated any positive value as famine: starvation
mortality took the whole `d` as its hazard argument, fertility suppression took the whole `d`, the
migration Exit valve took the whole `d`. Nothing distinguished "the harvest was 15 % thin and people
ate less" from "there is no food". There was no concept of an exceptional cause at all, and no
disaster mechanism of any kind existed in the tree. **CONFIRMED** (git, this lane:
`Sim.Core/Systems/Disaster/` does not exist at `45046eb`; it is added by `f4ae48f`).

**1.2 Shock integration had no adaptation.** **CONFIRMED** (CR-015 §2.1 item 6, the mandate's own
words). A single 0.30-deficit decade removed roughly 59 % of Libur. The kernel is linear in `d`:
adult starvation over a decade is `1 − e^{−0.12·d·10}` = 30 % at `d = 0.30`, and fertility
suppression `max(0, 1 − 3.0·d)` removes 90 % of a decade's births at the same `d`. Neither form has
any notion that a sustained ration cut of 15–25 % is survivable. **CONFIRMED** (CR-015 §2.2
derivations, re-derived from the shipped constants by lane A and reproduced to the digit).

**1.3 Flight was unbounded in `dt` and multiplied by the number of exits.** **CONFIRMED** (CR-015
§1 N4, §2.2; the cascade evidence at `docs/t4.21-evidence/cascade-report.md` §3 G). The live form
summed a per-destination flight desire over destinations, so the total leaving a source scaled with
how MANY reachable viable destinations it had: at Libur, `Σ_j damping·viability = 4.384` with
viability exactly 1.0 for all eleven neighbours — a 4.4× magnitude multiplier, in direct
contradiction of ADR-012's own claim that viability "only redistributes WHERE". The per-bucket
desire `K·d·dt = 2.4 × 0.297 = 0.71` per destination then saturated the `min(1, ·)` overdraw clamp
for cohorts 2–7 (55.6 % of the settlement). The clamp was the only bound, and a clamp is not an
integration. **CONFIRMED** (the aggregate reproduces the measured 641 departures to within
1.2 persons — cascade report §3 G).

**1.4 Destination fan-in was "pinned empirically", not bounded.** **CONFIRMED** (CR-015 §1 N4
quoting `MigrationSystem.cs:93-95` on `45046eb`). Multiple sources sharing one destination summed
their pair caps; the collective inflow was bounded by a comment and a regression test on N = 12, not
by a mechanism. Nothing anywhere asked whether the destination could FEED the arrivals. Vacancy did
not exist as a concept.

**1.5 There was no population-cap semantics at all.** **CONFIRMED** (CR-015 §1 N3, quoting
`docs/t4.20-food-semantics.md:178-179` and `docs/queue.md:1358-1359` on `45046eb`:
`FoodSupportedPopulation` and `FoodConstrainedGrowth` "were **not** added: both presuppose a
population-cap semantics on which no ruling exists"). A settlement could grow past what its food
influx feeds, and only discover it through a deficit.

**The observable, in one line.** Libur t117 drew a 0.4th-percentile harvest decade, ran a deficit of
0.297207, and on t118 went **809 → 69** — a 91.5 % loss in one turn, of which 641 people (79.2 % of
the opening population) walked out — and then refilled **1213 into 69** on t119, a 17.6× inflow,
before re-crashing twice. **CONFIRMED** (lane A, reproduced exactly on its own `val-before` tree
@ `45046eb`; all four anchor numbers matched the director's telemetry).

## 4. NEW FAMINE SEMANTICS

**4.1 The four-state ladder.** **CONFIRMED** (code read on `64a3f2f`, this lane;
`Sim.Core/State/FoodState.cs`). With `d` the nominal deficit and `a` the adaptation boundary:

| state | condition |
| --- | --- |
| NORMAL | `d = 0` |
| STRESS | `0 < d ≤ a` — the cut is absorbed; nobody dies of it |
| SEVERE | `d > a` — adaptation exhausted; starvation on the unabsorbed remainder |
| FAMINE | `d > 0` **AND** a cause: a famine-class disaster APPLIED to the harvest that produced `d`, or deliberate productive abandonment (farming = 0 **and** herding = 0) |

The state is **derived**, never stored, never serialized — a static recomputed on demand, crossing
no isolation boundary. **DESIGN DECISION** (the mandate's "no serialized famine-stress state unless
a persistent regime is demonstrated"; none was).

**Ordinary weather is never famine, however bad the draw.** **CONFIRMED** (lane A, `val-after` @
`64a3f2f`: Libur's t117 shock classifies **SEVERE, reason None**, with `disasterRowPresent` false
and `abandoned` false; lane B, `val-b-after`: the one SEVERE settlement-turn in its whole 20-seed
sweep, seed 3 S11 turn 521, likewise classifies Severe/None on a prior-turn harvest multiplier of
0.2988). FAMINE never fires without a shortfall either: a disaster a rich granary absorbs is not a
famine. **CONFIRMED** (code read, this lane).

**4.2 The effective deficit — a dead-zone adaptation form.** **CONFIRMED** (code read +
lane A arithmetic + lane C's pinned null arm):

```
d_eff = 0                        for d ≤ a
d_eff = (d − a) / (1 − a)        for d > a          (exactly 1 at d = 1)
d_eff = d                        in FAMINE          (no adaptation; the whole deficit starves)
```

`a` = **0.20**, shipped. **DESIGN DECISION** with a stated physiological frame (CR-015 §6.1,
`sim.json foodState._doc`): sustained ration cuts of ~15–25 % are survivable without excess
mortality; 40–70 % cuts carried excess mortality; the band's midpoint taken. `a` is **TUNE**, and
the loader refuses values outside [0,1).

**One boundary carries the kernel semantics.** `a` is simultaneously the dead zone of `d_eff` and
the SEVERE threshold (`θ_sev := a`), so the label predicts the kernel's response rather than
decorating it. **DESIGN DECISION** (`FoodState.cs` header; ADR-024).

**Who reads `d_eff` and who does not.** `d_eff` feeds **exactly two** things: starvation mortality
and fertility suppression. Every other deficit reader — the rebound release gate, destination
viability, happiness, appropriation, demote-first, and migration's own flight hazard — keeps the
**NOMINAL** `d`. **CONFIRMED** (code read on `64a3f2f`, this lane; `sim.json foodState._doc`;
lane A verified the migration side by reproducing the flight fraction from the nominal 0.295957).

**Worked, on the shipped tree.** Libur's t117 nominal 0.295957 gives
`d_eff = (0.295957 − 0.20)/0.80 = 0.11994618964514252` — bit-for-bit what the telemetry carries.
Starvation hazard becomes `0.12 × 0.11995 = 0.0143936/yr` (13.4 % over a decade) instead of
`0.12 × 0.295957 = 0.0355148/yr` (30.0 %); fertility suppression becomes
`max(0, 1 − 3 × 0.11995) = 0.640` instead of 0.112. **CONFIRMED** (lane A, `val-after`).

**4.3 The famine-class disaster — `DisasterSystem` + `DisasterRow`, schema v25.**
**CONFIRMED** (code read + git, this lane). One kind (crop failure). It multiplies the same two
FOOD paths harvest weather multiplies — farming and herding/fishing — and **never** extraction,
**never** stores. It reads no population and no deficit: like weather it cannot decide famine;
`FoodState` decides, from the balance. Onset is the exact integration `P = 1 − exp(−λ·dt)`; the
turn multiplier is `1 − severity·min(remaining, dt)/dt`, so a five-year failure costs the same total
food at dt 10 and at dt 0.5. `RemainingYears` persists by dimension. Constants: `durationYears` 5.0,
`severityMin` 0.75, `severityMax` 1.0, all inside stated historical bands.

**IT SHIPS INERT.** `disaster.hazardPerYear = 0.0`. **CONFIRMED** (this lane, read directly from
`Sim.Data/content/sim.json:235` on `64a3f2f`; lane C confirmed the same value in its own worktree;
lane B confirmed `disasterRowPresent` false across 32,052 settlement-turns and
`disasterAppliedMultiplier` exactly 1 everywhere). The derived rate is 0.01 — one famine-class local
crop failure per settlement per century, from a stated pre-modern European reference class. It was
ARMED by T4.21-4, measured to break three frozen gates, and returned to 0.0 by T4.21-7 pending the
director's ruling. **The mechanism is complete and tested; only the rate is unset.** See §15.

**4.4 What this buys, stated as the semantics and not as an outcome.** The ladder separates
"the harvest was thin" from "there is no food" by CAUSE, not by magnitude. **INFERRED** from the
mechanism: on a world where no disaster fires and nobody abandons, FAMINE is unreachable and the
exceptional arm is dead code paths that the test suite exercises and play does not. That inference
is confirmed in play — **CONFIRMED** (lane B: **0 FAMINE settlement-turns in 39,852** across all
fifteen AFTER emit-session runs; histogram Normal 39,659 / Stress 192 / Severe 1 / Famine 0).

## 5. NEW MIGRATION ARCHITECTURE

The mandate's pipeline is stress → hazard → bounded source outflow → destination shares → bounded
destination inflow, with HOW MANY separated from WHERE.

**5.1 The flight fraction is an exactly integrated hazard.** **CONFIRMED** (code read on
`64a3f2f`, this lane; `MigrationSystem.FlightFractionOf`):

```
φ = 1 − exp(−profile × K × ω × d × dt)        K = BaseRatePerYear × FamineFlightFactor = 0.24/yr
```

on the **nominal** deficit `d`. It is exactly 0 when there is no deficit or no open exit — "die at
home", by branch AND by value, preserving D-021. It is bounded below the bucket's count by
construction, so the old overdraw clamp is now a backstop rather than the only bound.
**CONFIRMED** (lane A reproduced the shipped `φ' = 0.449561969296023` at Libur t118 from the shipped
constants: `1 − exp(−1 × 0.24 × 0.8405514 × 0.295957 × 10)`).

**5.2 EXIT OPENNESS ω — the best exit, not the sum over exits.** **CONFIRMED** (code read:
`MigrationPlan.ExitOpenness`, `ω_i = max_{j≠i} damping_ij × viability_j ∈ [0,1]`).
**This is the fix for the 4.4× magnitude multiplier.** How many leave depends on the source's stress
and on how OPEN its best exit is; it does not depend on how many exits there are.

**5.3 Destination SHARES distribute WHERE.** **CONFIRMED** (code read:
`ShareNormaliser Z_i = Σ_{j≠i} damping_ij × viability_j`). The sum that used to set the QUANTITY now
sets only the DISTRIBUTION. **Destination count no longer multiplies how many leave.**
**CONFIRMED** in play (lane A: at t119 the same **eleven** source settlements drain in both trees
and the same mechanism fires; BEFORE they shed 1276 people, AFTER **96**).

**5.4 The planner is a pure function.** `MigrationSystem.Plan` was extracted as a pure planner
consumed by `Step`, bit-identically, before any behaviour changed (`517cfdb`). This is what lets the
observer read the plan's bounds without a second implementation. **CONFIRMED** (git, this lane).

## 6. NEW DESTINATION ARCHITECTURE

**VACANCY ≠ ATTRACTIVENESS, made literal.** Three separate questions, three separate mechanisms.
**DESIGN DECISION** (the mandate; ADR-025).

**6.1 CAN it accept at all — viability, unchanged.** ADR-012's viability mechanism, its collapse
teeth and its refusal of floors and quantization are preserved exactly. **CONFIRMED** (CR-015 §6.5;
the ADR-012 amendment line appended at `f4a5196` amends only the Exit-valve paragraph's claim that
viability "only redistributes WHERE", not the mechanism).

**6.2 HOW MANY can flow through the gap channel — basin caps at both ends.** **CONFIRMED** (code
read on `64a3f2f`, this lane; `MigrationSystem` header §T2.8(a) as amended by ADR-025 §2.3). The
T2.8 pair physics, applied to a whole basin. For a destination `j` with basin
`B_j = {i : pair-capped gap desire i→j > 0}` of two or more sources, `j` is pooled with its sources:

```
P_pool = P_j + Σ P_i,   R_pool = R_j + Σ R_i
M*_j^in  = max(0, R_j × P_pool / R_pool − P_j)       destination cap: pair-capped inflow ≤ f × M*_j^in
M*_i^out = max(0, P_i − R_i × P_pool / R_pool)       source cap (the mirror), on dest-scaled outflow
```

Single-pair basins SKIP, so every single-source-single-destination rig is **bit-identical**. **No
new constant** — `f`, `R`, `P` are T2.8's. This replaces the summed pair caps, which reached
`(k+1)/2 × f × m*` and were measured at 6.1–6.4× at Libur t119. **PROBABLE** as to that 6.1–6.4×
(cascade evidence, prior agent); **CONFIRMED** as to the form (code).

**6.3 HOW MANY the destination can ABSORB — the VACANCY bound on TOTAL inflow.** **CONFIRMED**
(code read; `MigrationSystem` header §3.5c / ADR-025 §2.4):

```
V_j    = FoodHeadroom.Vacancy(prev, j) = max(0, N_lim,j − N_j)     in adult-equivalents
cap_j  = (1 − exp(−k·dt)) × V_j                                     k = ln2/10 per year
```

applied to the destination's TOTAL desired inflow across **both** channels (gap and flight)
together. **Capacity limits absorption; attractiveness only distributes.** `k` is
`headroomRelaxationPerYear` = 0.0693147180559945 — **one law, one constant, shared with the
demographic headroom cap** (§7). **DESIGN DECISION**, chosen not derived, inside the director's
frame "the gap halves per decade". **CONFIRMED** (this lane, read from `sim.json:91`).

**6.4 Measured effect.** **CONFIRMED**:

| metric | BEFORE `45046eb` | AFTER `64a3f2f` | lane / world |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| max inflow fraction | 17.5797 | **0.175365** | A, playtest seed 42 |
| max population gain fraction | 21.0290 | **0.248434** | A, playtest seed 42 |
| settlement-turns with inflow > 50 % | 6 | **0** | A |
| max inflow fraction (worst of 10 seeds) | 188.9 % | **24.4 %** | B, seeds 1–10, no orders |
| max population gain (worst of 10 seeds) | 229.6 % | **29.3 %** | B, seeds 1–10, no orders |

Lane B checked that these are genuine migration surges and not founding artefacts: every settlement
involved has `foundedTurn 0`. **CONFIRMED** (lane B, `val-b-before`/`val-b-after`). Lane B calls the
destination bounds "the biggest behavioural change, and it is a large one" — carried here as its
finding, not softened.

**6.5 The vacancy bound is what actually stopped the refill, and it is observable.** At Libur t118
the destination's own vacancy cap is **0** (food limit 403.57 against 837 residents, headroom 0),

### 15.2 G1 — THE HARVEST-WEATHER DECADE VARIANCE. **UNRESOLVED; DIRECTOR ONLY.**

**The finding, with its derivation.** `HarvestWeatherSystem.cs:42-45` holds the log-deviation at
STATIONARY variance σ² for every dt (`ρ_AR = e^{−dt/τ}`, innovation `σ√(1 − ρ²)`) — which is the
deliberately ratified T3.4b/T3.4c invariant that stops the era table altering the climate. But
`ProductionSystem.cs:239, :306` apply `exp(x − σ²/2)` to the WHOLE turn's output, and a turn's
harvest is the turn-MEAN of the yearly multipliers:

```
Var(mean over T) = σ² · g(T/τ),   g(T/τ) = 2(τ/T)[1 − (τ/T)(1 − e^{−T/τ})]
g = 0.427 at T = 10 y (τ = 3):   σ_decade    = 0.2936 × √0.427 = 0.192
g = 0.948 at T = 0.5 y:          σ_half-year = 0.286
```

5th-percentile decade reads 0.59 as shipped where the yearly σ/τ imply 0.72;

variance, an ordinary SEVERE decade produces a LARGER dt-10 deficit at a ρ = 1.3 settlement than a
famine-class disaster does — and the era table alters the decade-scale climate, the exact hazard the
code comment names. **CONFIRMED** (CR-015 §6.4 / §2.3 F1; the derivation re-read on `64a3f2f` by
this lane).

"stochastic and unclamped"; law 3 says the output should carry the turn-mean's variance. S8 §3
escalates such a conflict; it does not let a packet resolve it by choosing. Applying `g` would also
be a fourth behavioural cause moving every golden, and must never share an attribution step with
T4.21-1's layout-only move. **DESIGN DECISION** (CR-015 §6.4).

**Options on record.** (a) **LEAVE** — the standing state. (b) **APPLY `g`**:
