## B. THE INSTRUMENT DISAGREEMENT — FIXED (see `corridors.json`, commit `1fad8e9`)

`corridors.json` carried `[0.15, 0.6]`; `CalibrationBatteryTests` hard-coded `[1.40, 1.80]`; the
nightly `jq` read only the band, and therefore gated on a corridor the project had explicitly
quarantined. **Neither band moved** — the target corridor and the recorded deviation are
different objects and both are legitimate. What was wrong is that the nightly did not know the
quarantine existed.

**The fix:** quarantine state lives in `corridors.json` beside the band
(`{active, window, owner, reason, history}`), and **both instruments read that one field**. A
quarantined corridor is reported `QUARANTINED` by the nightly **with its measured range printed
every night** — it does not gate, and it does not go invisible. The battery keeps its per-seed
teeth in both directions, unchanged; only their source moved from a constant to the data file.

**Red-proven, both arms, against a COMMITTED baseline (`1fad8e9`):**

| arm | nightly density gate | `CalibrationBatteryTests` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `active: true` (shipped) | PASS — reported, not gated | 6/6 passed |
| `active: false` | **FAIL** — 20/20 outside `[0.15, 0.6]` | **2 failed**, `declares no ACTIVE quarantine…` |

Tree restored and verified identical to the baseline by `git diff --stat`.

### The fix immediately revealed what it was hiding

With density correctly quarantined **the nightly still fails** — on
`migrationGrossPerDecade`, **seed 9 at 0.00098 against a 0.001 floor, 2 % under**. Isolated:
density-only PASS, liveness PASS, migration FAIL. That reading had been buried under the false
density red for eleven nights. It is **not** dispositioned here — it is T3.12b's third signal.

## D. THE DECOMPOSITION — A GENUINELY NEW FACT (folded into T3.12b)

Stated before interpretation, per §7.15. Population and arable, separately, across 20 seeds:

| | min | max | spread | CV |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| population | 81,160 | 132,280 | 1.63× | 0.114 |
| arable km² | 69,117 | 90,193 | 1.30× | 0.063 |
| density | 1.1428 | 1.6501 | 1.44× | 0.097 |

- **corr(density, population) = +0.858**
- **corr(density, arable) = +0.018**
- corr(population, arable) = +0.527

**The across-seed SPREAD is a NUMERATOR signal — the first time in five occurrences that this
metric moved for a population reason rather than a denominator one** (CR-002, CR-003, T3.5b,
T3.6b, T3.8 were all denominator stories). Seed 3 makes it concrete: highest arable in the
sweep, mid population, lowest density.

This decomposes the **spread**, not the **level**; the level remains CR-002's denomination
shift. Folded into T3.12b as evidence, per the ruling.
