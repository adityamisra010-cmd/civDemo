## Mutation testing — bounds stated before the sweep (ADR-015 §7.1)

- Clean filtered-subset baseline measured immediately before the sweep and recorded.
- Per-mutant bound **5× that baseline**, hard timeout, killed at the bound.
- A mutant exceeding the bound is recorded `hangs; non-termination under this mutation`.
- **A run that never built is not a hang** — read the log before filing non-termination (§7.9; the
  T3.4b test-power lens caught itself doing exactly this and checked).
- One worktree per verifying agent, never shared (§6).

## Verification discipline

- **No finding is actionable before its verdict returns** (§6). On T3.4b, verification refuted or
  materially cut down **eight** findings, in most of which the measurement was exactly right and the
  conclusion drawn from it was wrong.
- **§7.10 on every finding before relaying it:** name the ratified item that REQUIRES the property
  claimed to be broken, and quote it. "This looks wrong" is not a conflict, and a bound is not the
  property you expected it to be. Lens agreement corroborates the MEASUREMENT only — lenses briefed
  on one question share its framing.
- **§7.12 on every claim you write down:** a property asserted about your own artifact is a
  measurement you owe, not a summary you may write.
