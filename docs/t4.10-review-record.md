### Why this is a STOP, not a re-pin

The migration reading did not drift within its prior scatter — it collapsed to roughly a third of
its T3.12 range, and the failure mode changed from "one small-world seed 2% under an absolute floor"
(a corridor-specification question, per the existing quarantine's own `reason` field,
`Sim.Data/content/corridors.json:88`) to "every single seed of twenty, 38–81% under." That is a
different phenomenon, not the same one measured again. The density mean also moved a full 12.3% in
one direction. Both moved together, in the same direction (down), between the same two measurement
points.

**The only behavior-changing merge between the T3.12 baseline and this sweep's base commit
(`ba96b1c`) is T4.2** (grain spoilage + granary capacity bounding, `store-bounding`). T4.3 is
schema-only (no system reads/writes the new tables) and T4.9 is docs-only — neither can move a
simulated quantity. T4.2 is therefore the **named candidate**, not a guess pulled from nowhere, but
it has **not been bisected** — no experiment in this packet isolates spoilage/granary bounding as
the mechanism, and this packet's fence explicitly forbids touching T4.2's constants or reopening its
certified reds to test that hypothesis.

Per this packet's explicit **STOP condition 3** ("the 20-seed sweep reveals a mechanism-level
failure requiring a design decision"): **STOPPING HERE.** No `corridors.json` re-pin, revision, or
retirement was applied. Re-pinning a window to absorb this drift without understanding its cause
would repeat exactly the mistake T3.12 itself named and refused to make on the density corridor
("a floor validated on two seeds is not a floor... re-pinning now would chase a drift nobody has
measured").

**Recommendation for the director's ruling, not enacted here:** bisect whether T4.2's store bounding
(spoilage discount on stored grain, or the granary capacity ceiling, or both) is suppressing the
per-capita food-surplus differentials that both `MigrationSystem`'s gap-closing formula and the
density corridor's demography-driven numerator depend on. If confirmed, this is a genuine downstream
consequence of a certified T4.2 mechanism working as designed, not a defect in T4.2 itself — the
corridors would need re-derivation against the new regime, which is exactly the kind of design
decision this packet's fence reserves for director ruling rather than in-packet improvisation.

## ITEM 4 — WITHDRAWN COMPARATIVE-ADVANTAGE CRITERION: STOP CONDITION 4 TRIGGERED.

`docs/m4-spec.md:271` lists the withdrawn comparative-advantage criterion as part of T4.10's
authoritative scope with no conditional hedge. However, direct reading of
`docs/t4.1b-review-record.md:394-441` shows the criterion's precondition is broken by a genuine
deposit-model defect, not by anything T3.10-migrated work can fix: the moisture-channel deposit field
saturates at every settlement site (siting selects for water access, which is exactly where the
moisture channel reads a flat 1.0), so extraction differences that would let comparative advantage
emerge never arise for any moisture-channel good (livestock, timber, fiber, hides). The record names
three possible fix shapes (explicit `depositSpread` per good; sample the catchment instead of the
site cell; sample an unsaturated field) and states explicitly: **"All three are design changes to
the deposit model and none is mine to take. Owner: the director"**
(`t4.1b-review-record.md:429-431`).

There is no existing skipped/disabled test to reactivate for this criterion — per direct read, it
was withdrawn before implementation (`docs/milestones.md:229-236`) and no corridor test for it has
ever been built (confirmed: `GoodsTests.cs:79` and `ProductionTests.cs:174,183` only cover the
founding-endowment precondition, not the emergence/specialization corridor itself).

Restoring this criterion therefore requires authoring a new test **against a deposit model that does
not yet support it** — i.e. it requires a new economic mechanism (one of the three listed fixes),
not migrated calibration work. Per this packet's explicit **STOP condition 4**
("the comparative-advantage criterion requires a new economic mechanism rather than the migrated
work specified"): **STOPPING HERE.** No test was authored, no deposit-model change was made.
