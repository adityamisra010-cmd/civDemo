The solver's mathematics changed and the population of prices moved with it; the individual
goods chosen for the table happen to be clamped.

**Said explicitly, because it is the stronger reading and was nearly left implicit (director
ruling, Q2): the two worlds' sums move in OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS, and that is better evidence than
a shared shift would have been.** A common-mode artifact — a build difference, a stray global,
an environment effect, anything not the perturbation — would push both worlds the same way. It
cannot push one up 2.25 and the other down 0.12. Opposite signs are only producible by the
solver actually running on two differently-conditioned states. The weaker-looking arm is the
better-evidenced one.

**And the shape of that is itself a finding: P1's evidence had to CHANGE SHAPE mid-proof, and
the reason is escalation 2 operating inside a red proof.** The per-good reading was planned; it
came back invisible; the aggregate was substituted, and only then did P1 show. Nothing was
wrong with the perturbation or the plan — the three goods happened to rest on a band edge, and
**a clamped price cannot show a perturbation that would otherwise move it.** This is §7.5's
hazard ("never assert on a quantity resting against its own limit") arriving UNINVITED. §7.5 is
written as a rule about how to author a guard; escalation 2 turns it into a PROPERTY OF THE
WORLD that degrades guards nobody authored carelessly. Filed as Q-B's fifth prediction
(`docs/queue.md`) — the first Q-B prediction that is about this project's instruments rather
than its simulation.

That is a smaller claim than "closed the blocking gap", and it is the one the measurements
support.

### GOV-2 §5 — THE KERNEL CLONE-SIZE CLAIM, DISCHARGED

The claim under test (`docs/m0-kernel-spec.md` §3.2): *"At turn start the kernel clones Prev →
Next (full copy; at M0–M9 scale this is a few MB…)"*. GOV-2 §5 recorded the measurement as owed

state-footprint output at all — so this packet added one rather than reporting an unmeasured
number. `TurnExecutor` already brackets exactly `prev.Clone()` with
`GC.GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread`, so the clone phase's `allocatedBytes` IS the per-turn
clone cost; bench now divides it by turns and prints it beside the bucket row count.

**MEASURED, today (N = 12, 300 turns):**

```
state: bucket_rows 384, clone_bytes_per_turn 82096 (0.078 MiB)
```

- **384 bucket rows — DENSE CONFIRMED.** GOV-2 predicted 384 from code alone (12 × 1 culture ×
  1 religion × 2 classes × 16 cohorts). The bench agrees exactly. Dense founding is not an
  inference any more.
- **Clone cost: 82,096 B/turn = 0.078 MiB.** Against "a few MB" this is roughly **40× UNDER**
  the claim.

**Scaling, MEASURED not assumed** — `--settlements` varied and the slope read directly:

| settlements | bucket rows | clone bytes/turn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | 384 | 73,906 |
| 24 | 768 | 147,036 |

Linear: **≈ 6,094 B per settlement**, intercept ≈ 778 B. Essentially the whole clone scales with
settlement count. (N = 48 could not be founded — siting places only 47 of 48 at
`minSpacingKm 480`. Not a defect for this measurement; two points establish the line and the
third would have been a check, so it is reported as not-run rather than quietly dropped.)

**PROJECTION** — arithmetic, and labelled as such. `BucketRow` is 4 id fields + an int + a
`Conserved` long + 7 doubles = 84 B unpadded, 88 B with alignment, so today's 32 rows/settlement
are ~2,816 B of the measured 6,094, leaving ~3,278 B/settlement of everything else.

| scenario | bucket rows | clone/turn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **today** (M3, N = 12, 2 classes) | 384 | **0.078 MiB — MEASURED** |
| Charter late game, 800 settlements, D-018's 12 classes, 1 culture × 1 religion | 153,600 (**at the ~150k cap**) | ~16 MB |
| same, 4 cultures × 4 religions | ~2.46M | ~219 MB |

**VERDICT: CONFIRM for the milestones reached, NARROW for the rest.** The §3.2 claim is true
today with an enormous margin — 0.078 MiB is not "a few MB", it is two orders under it — and it
would still hold at 800 settlements if classes and cultures stayed where they are (~4.9 MB). It
does **not** survive D-018's class roster: at the Spine's own ratified ~150k bucket cap the clone
is order 16 MB, and plural cultures/religions take it to order 200 MB. So the claim's stated
range "M0–M9" is wrong at the far end; what it actually covers is "while buckets stay small",
and buckets are exactly what D-018 and plural cultures grow.

**NOT AMENDED HERE, and that is deliberate.** `m0-kernel-spec.md` is inside the M0 freeze
perimeter (`spine-s8-governance-freeze.md:15,202`), so the amendment mechanism is a
director-ruled ADR, not a T3.11 edit. This packet owed the measurement and has produced it; the
wording change is the director's to authorise. Recorded, with the numbers, for that ruling.
