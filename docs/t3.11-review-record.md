### What the driven golden actually ADDS — measured, not claimed (§1.1)

The spec required this to be measured rather than assumed, and the measurement contradicts the
convenient answer:

**The existing founded golden ALREADY catches both perturbations.** Its hash changes under P1
and under P2. T3.6's finding (50 non-grain series off 1.0 on a no-order world) predicted this,
and this run confirms it: 156 of 156 non-grain rows are off 1.0 on the founded world too. The
blocking gap as originally written — "ADR-016 changed the solver's mathematics while leaving all
three goldens byte-identical" — was already **half closed by T3.2b/T3.5b** spreading production
across sectors, before this packet existed.

So the driven golden's marginal contribution is **NOT price sensitivity.** It is:

1. **Flow sensitivity** — the founded world moves zero goods, so no existing golden can regress
   on the trade solver at all. The driven world moves 46 units across 23 rows and 2 goods.
2. **A second, differently-conditioned witness to the price solver** — the two goldens' price
   sums move in opposite directions under P1, so they are not redundant copies of one reading.
3. **Coverage of the order path itself** — the anti-vacuity guard fails if `SectorAllocation`
   delivery ever silently stops landing, which no golden previously observed.

That is a smaller claim than "closed the blocking gap", and it is the one the measurements
support.
