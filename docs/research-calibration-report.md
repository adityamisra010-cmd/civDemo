## 4. The multipliers against the content

The content fixes only the Eureka part. In A9, the full Eureka entitlement is 7.91 % of the workload (2.66 % from conditions evaluable today). No modifier or
foreign-exposure source exists, so their sizes are assumptions:

- optimised = 0.85 (university-style) − Eureka − foreign exposure. With every Eureka fired, the stated 0.756 implies foreign exposure worth 1.49 % of the final-Age workload;
- lower development = 1 − ½ × Eureka = **0.960** from the content, against the stated 0.985 (½ × the evaluable-today share alone gives 0.987).

The proposal's derivation of its two multipliers is not reproducible from the stated assumptions alone; the
tables above use them as given. Under the content-derived lower-development multiplier the final Age at the
40M scenario takes 829 turns instead.
