## 1. The two models

- **Research Points (ADR-030):** RP per turn = 0.08 × population^0.699 — per strategic
  turn, never multiplied by dtYears; it funds the Technology target only (ADR-043). Anchors: 100 → 2.000 (stated 2), 1,000 → 10.002 (stated 10).
- **Civics Points (ADR-054; ADR-043):** CP per turn = the Research Point base, 0.08 × population^0.699 — the SAME function of the same population, never multiplied by dtYears (the
  Director's binding rule of 2026-10-27: base RP = base CP at every turn length); banked in integer milli-CP
  without cap or loss. Both read the average population (populationAverageTurns 2: the mean of the previous and the current turn-start population).
  CP fund the Civics target only. The 480 civics (4,846,450 in all) are paid in CP, not RP: the RP workloads below still list them by Age, so they overstate the RP
  workload by exactly that amount.
- **Cost:** BaseCost = U × K^magnitude, U = 92.4, K = 2.0; EffectiveCost = max(0.2 × BaseCost, BaseCost ×
  modifiers); acceleration credit (Eureka + foreign exposure) ≤ 0.4 × BaseCost per node, capped at the
  remaining EffectiveCost.
- **U calibration anchor (A1 = 5,000, gate ruling 1):** RP(5,000) = 30.808 RP/turn, so a magnitude-0 node (92.4 RP) takes 2.999 turns (stated: 3).

## 4. The multipliers against the content

The content fixes only the Eureka part. In A9, the full Eureka entitlement is 5.06 % of the workload (1.45 % from conditions evaluable today). No modifier or
foreign-exposure source exists, so their sizes are assumptions:

- optimised = 0.85 (university-style) − Eureka − foreign exposure. With every Eureka fired, the stated 0.756 implies foreign exposure worth 4.34 % of the final-Age workload;
- lower development = 1 − ½ × Eureka = **0.975** from the content, against the stated 0.985 (½ × the evaluable-today share alone gives 0.993).

The proposal's derivation of its two multipliers is not reproducible from the stated assumptions alone; the
tables above use them as given. Under the content-derived lower-development multiplier the final Age at the
40M scenario takes 1264 turns instead.
